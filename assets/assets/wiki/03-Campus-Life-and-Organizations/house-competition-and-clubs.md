# Club Activities & HOUSE Competition

## HOUSE Competition System

**HOUSE** stands for **Honoring Our Unique Social Environment**. The program is named after Sohei Nakayama, the creator of IUJ. All students belong to one of 8 houses, each named after an IUJ Founding Father or Chairman.

3–4 competitions per term accumulate points toward the **HOUSE Champion** title at the end of the academic year.

**Head of HOUSE (2025–2026):** Rakoto Andriamihaja, Carole Miora — miorara@iuj.ac.jp
