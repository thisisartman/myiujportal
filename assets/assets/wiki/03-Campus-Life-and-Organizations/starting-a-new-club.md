# Starting a New Club at IUJ

There is no stated policy or formal guidelines for starting a club at IUJ — which means it is really easy. The process is informal and student-driven.

## Steps to Start a Club

1. **Inform the GSO-EC and OSS** of your intent so they can support you. The OSS will update the official Club Listings to include your new club.

2. **Create a poster** clearly stating what the club is about, what is required of members (fees, meeting frequency, goals, obligations). Post it on dorm bulletin boards and the brown boards outside the OSS office.

3. **Announce the first meeting** on the poster with date, time, and location. Give at least 3 days for people to see it and adjust their schedules. Also post on the @iuj page and add the event to the shared calendar.

4. **Personally invite** students, faculty, and staff — posters and online notices alone are rarely enough to bring people together.

5. **Do not mass-email** the entire IUJ address list to advertise your club. You may invite a select few by email, but campus-wide club advertisements via email are not appropriate.

6. **Budget requests:** If you need funding, contact the GSO-EC. Budget requests are typically taken in mid-Winter Term; the fiscal year runs April 1 through March 31. The EC sometimes has discretionary funds to help new clubs get started — but much can be done without a budget.

7. **Have fun!**

## Notes

- The OSS and GSO-EC are your primary points of support for getting a club off the ground
- Existing club listings are maintained by OSS: see [Campus Clubs & Activities](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/future-students/campus-clubs-and-events/activities/)
- All clubs are student-run; there is no mandatory faculty advisor requirement
