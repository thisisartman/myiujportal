# Dream Campus — Academic Affairs System

## Overview

**Dream Campus** (`dcweb.iuj.ac.jp`) is IUJ's academic affairs and course registration system. It is separate from the MyIUJ! portal and requires its own login. You access it via the **Course** tab in MyIUJ! or directly at:

**URL:** [https://dcweb.iuj.ac.jp/Portal/](https://dcweb.iuj.ac.jp/Portal/)

---

## Logging In

1. Go to [https://dcweb.iuj.ac.jp/Portal/](https://dcweb.iuj.ac.jp/Portal/)
2. Enter your **IUJ ID** — do **not** include `@iuj.ac.jp` (just the username part)
3. Enter your **Password**
4. Click Login

> **Account lockout:** 10 consecutive wrong password attempts will lock your account. Contact the academic affairs office to unlock it.

> **Auto-logout:** Sessions expire after **60 minutes** of inactivity.

---

## What You Can Do

- **Course registration** — register for courses each term
- **Student Information Web Page**
  - GSIR students: access your GSIR academic records
  - GSIM students: access your GSIM academic records
- **Syllabus search** — browse and search course syllabi for any term

## System Requirements

Dream Campus was designed for older browsers but works in modern ones. Officially listed requirements (legacy):

- Windows: Internet Explorer 7.0+ or Firefox 2.0+
- Mac: Safari 3.0+ or Firefox 2.0+
