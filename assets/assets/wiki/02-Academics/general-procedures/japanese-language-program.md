# Japanese Language Program (JLP)

The JLP emphasises developing communication skills (listening and speaking prioritised, with reading and writing also taught) across all levels. Courses are divided into two tracks: **Main Track** and **Light Load**.

The JLP strongly advises incoming students to begin studying Japanese before arriving at IUJ to maximise results.

## Course Tracks

### Main Track
Designed to develop comprehensive Japanese skills including Kanji. Classes in Elementary, Intermediate, and Upper-Intermediate meet **4 times per week**; Advanced meets **3 times per week**.

**Levels:** Elementary 1–3 → Intermediate 1–3 → Upper-Intermediate 1–3 → Advanced 1–3

> Many alumni report that completion of **Intermediate 3 or above** is the minimum level required for professional use (internship, research, business).

### Light Load Track (Basic Japanese)
For students wanting to experience Japanese without heavy load on content courses. Classes meet **2 times per week**. Focus on oral communication; Kanji not taught due to time constraints.

**Levels:** Basic 1–3 → Basic 4–6

### Japanese for Zero Beginners
Four 90-min classes offered during the second module (weeks 9–10) of the Winter term. For students with no or little Japanese knowledge, or those who missed/want to repeat the Intensive Japanese Program (IJP).

## Proficiency Outcomes

| Course Completed | JLPT (December test) | OPI (Oral Proficiency Interview) | Notes |
|------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------|-------|
| Advanced 3 | N2 | Advanced Low/Mid | Requires strong kanji background + preparation |
| Upper-Intermediate 3 | N3 or N2 | Advanced Low or above | Requires strong kanji background + preparation |
| Intermediate 3 | N3 | Intermediate High | Requires continued independent rigorous study |
| Elementary 3 | N4 | Intermediate Low/Mid | With sufficient JLPT preparation |
| Basic 3 | N5 | Novice Mid/High | With ~100 kanji independent study |
| Basic 6 | N4 | Intermediate Low | With ~350 kanji independent study |

## Entry Requirements

Students wishing to take **Elementary Japanese 1** must pass a qualifying test covering basic linguistic knowledge of Japanese. Those who cannot prepare in advance may start with a **4-Day Intensive Class** (see Intensive Japanese Program), but must be aware this requires significant effort.
