# Japanese Courses — Full Course List

All Japanese courses are offered through CLEAR (Center for Language Education And Research). Students are placed into the appropriate level based on a qualifying test taken before the semester starts.

---

## Main Track

Designed for students who want to develop solid Japanese language proficiency alongside their graduate studies.

### Elementary Japanese 1 / 2 / 3
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring (one per term)
- **Hours:** 4 periods per week
- **Credit:** 1 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 35 students
- **Prerequisite:** Qualifying test placement
- **Focus:** Hiragana, katakana, basic grammar, survival communication; kanji introduced progressively
- **Sequence:** Complete all three to reach A2 level

### Intermediate Japanese 1 / 2 / 3
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring
- **Hours:** 4 periods per week
- **Credit:** 1 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 35 students
- **Prerequisite:** Elementary Japanese 3 or qualifying test placement
- **Focus:** Consolidating grammar, expanding vocabulary, reading short texts, practical conversation
- **Sequence:** Complete all three to reach B1 level

### Upper-Intermediate Japanese 1 / 2 / 3
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring
- **Hours:** 4 periods per week
- **Credit:** 1 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 35 students
- **Prerequisite:** Intermediate Japanese 3 or qualifying test placement
- **Focus:** Complex grammar, formal writing, discussion and presentation skills
- **Sequence:** Complete all three to approach B2 level

### Advanced Japanese 1 / 2 / 3
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring
- **Hours:** 3 periods per week
- **Credit:** 1 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 15 students
- **Prerequisite:** Upper-Intermediate Japanese 3 or qualifying test placement
- **Focus:** Academic and professional Japanese, debate, nuanced reading and writing
- **Note:** Smaller class size to allow intensive practice

---

## Light-Load Track

Designed for students with heavier academic schedules or those seeking slower, more gradual progress.

### Basic Japanese 1 / 2 / 3
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring
- **Hours:** 2 periods per week
- **Credit:** 0.5 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 30 students
- **Prerequisite:** Qualifying test placement (zero to very beginner level)
- **Focus:** Hiragana, katakana, essential survival phrases; slower pacing than Elementary track

### Basic Japanese 4 / 5 / 6
- **Offered:** Fall / Winter / Spring
- **Hours:** 2 periods per week
- **Credit:** 0.5 credit per term
- **Max enrolment:** 30 students
- **Prerequisite:** Basic Japanese 3 or qualifying test placement
- **Focus:** Continuation of Basic track; bridges toward Elementary 1 level

### Japanese for Zero Beginners
- **Offered:** Winter term only
- **Duration:** 4 classes (short intensive)
- **Credit:** 0 credits (non-credit)
- **Prerequisite:** None — complete beginners only
- **Focus:** Hiragana/katakana reading and writing; absolute survival basics
- **Purpose:** Preparation for the Basic Japanese 1 qualifying test

---

## Placement Qualifying Tests

Before each term, CLEAR administers a qualifying test to place students in the appropriate course. Students who have completed the previous course in the sequence are automatically eligible for the next level without retesting. Details on test dates are announced via the academic calendar.

---

## Notes

- Japanese courses are open to all enrolled graduate students regardless of program
- Credits earned count toward elective credits (check your program requirements)
- The Intensive Japanese Program (IJP) in September is a recommended preparation step for incoming students before the Fall term qualifying test — see [Intensive Japanese Program](intensive-japanese-program.md)
