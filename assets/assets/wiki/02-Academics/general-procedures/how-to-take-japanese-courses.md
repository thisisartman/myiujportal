# How to Take Japanese Courses

All Japanese Language Program (JLP) courses require students to follow a structured registration and qualifying process before enrolment is confirmed. Below are the steps and rules that apply.

---

## Application Steps for the Fall Term

1. **Watch the JLP orientation video** — CLEAR sends the link to all incoming students in mid-August. The video explains the course system, tracks, and expectations.
2. **Register during the official period** — Registration opens in August. If you are unsure which level to take, you may register for up to two courses. The coordinator will then send you qualifying test preparation details.
3. **Attend the Intensive Japanese Program (IJP)** — If you are a complete beginner, attend the 4-day IJP in mid-September before the Fall term. Registration instructions are emailed in mid-August. See [Intensive Japanese Program](intensive-japanese-program.md).
4. **Take and pass the qualifying test** — Placement is confirmed only after passing the qualifying test for the desired course.

---

## Important Rules

- **Enrollment cap:** Due to teaching resource constraints, course capacity may be limited. In cases of excess demand, regular IUJ students are given priority over exchange students. Among those who meet the deadline, applications are processed on a first-come basis.

- **Qualifying test is mandatory for all:** Every student must take and pass the qualifying test for the specific course they wish to enroll in. Passing the test for one course does not automatically qualify you for another. For example, passing the Elementary Japanese 1 qualifying test does not grant entry into Basic Japanese 1 — these are separate tests.

- **Re-testing after gaps:** Students who have not successfully completed the immediately preceding course — or who have taken a break of one or more terms (including summer break) — must take and pass the qualifying test again, even if they previously passed it.

- **Winter and Spring term tests:** Qualifying tests for Winter and Spring terms are administered just before the first day of each term. CLEAR announces dates by email before the end of the preceding term.

- **Exchange students and grading:** Short-term exchange students must consult the course coordinator during the change-of-registration period to select a grading option compatible with their home institution's system.

---

## Also Open to Faculty and Spouses

IUJ faculty members, as well as spouses of faculty members and students, may also enroll in Japanese Language Program courses including the IJP. Priority is given to enrolled IUJ and exchange students. Details available upon request from CLEAR.
