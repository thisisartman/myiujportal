# Center for Language Education and Research (CLEAR)

## About CLEAR

The Center for Language Education and Research (CLEAR) was established in April 2014 to offer high-quality foreign language teaching and education for IUJ students who aspire to play a leading role in the world.

CLEAR currently consists of two programs:
- **English Language Program (ELP)**
- **Japanese Language Program (JLP)**

Both programs provide practical language training for current and future academic and professional careers, with emphasis on research paper writing, oral presentations, and group discussions. CLEAR also provides language training to non-IUJ professionals during summer and other specific periods.

## Objectives

1. Provide high-quality foreign language programs to IUJ students, external course participants, and external institutions
2. Design and organize language courses and produce new/improved curricula and teaching materials
3. Enhance quality through research and study of second language acquisition and teaching skills

## Context

IUJ students come from approximately 35 countries. Since a large part of student life involves multicultural interactions, English and Japanese language education is vital. CLEAR also provides:
- Intensive English programs for Japanese businesses
- Programs for external university students and staff
- Workshops for elementary, junior high, and high school English teachers
- Japanese language education for overseas students
- Training courses for non-Japanese employees of Japanese companies
- Workshops for local community volunteers who teach Japanese

CLEAR was part of the "Top Global University Project" selected by MEXT (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science & Technology) from 2014.
