# International Relations Program (IRP)

## Two MA Programs Focusing on Theory, Methodology, and Policy Analysis

The International Relations Program (IRP) offers two **2-year MA degrees**:
1. **MA in International Relations (IR)**
2. **MA in Political Science (PS)**

### MA in International Relations
By taking the basic courses for the IR degree, students acquire the basic concepts and theories of international relations, empirical knowledge, and analytical skills — all necessary to understand the diverse aspects of international relations.

### MA in Political Science
In the PS program, students study topics such as comparative politics, political institutions, political theory and methodology, with a strong focus on methodology. They acquire sufficient methodological skills to analyze diverse issues in political science.

The IRP also offers various elective courses seeking to provide more advanced, specific, and up-to-date studies corresponding with the multi-disciplinary nature of the IR and PS fields.

**IRP students are expected to develop the capacity to analyze diverse global challenges and propose policy solutions for them.**

## What You Gain

### Knowledge and Skills
Gain sufficient knowledge and skills to understand, analyze, and evaluate issues in international relations.

### Career Opportunities
The knowledge and skills acquired allow pursuit of successful careers in:
- Governmental agencies
- Non-governmental organizations
- International organizations
- International businesses

### Global Network
Benefit from a network of graduates who occupy important positions in governments, international organizations, universities, research institutes, and media organizations across the world.

## Program Director's Message

**Vida Macikenaite, Assistant Professor and Program Director**

> "Students in the International Relations Program (IRP) can earn a master's degree in either International Relations or Political Science. Each equips students with an interdisciplinary understanding of the world, and develops their capacity to analyze diverse global challenges and propose policy solutions for them. With the knowledge and skills they acquire, they can pursue careers in governmental agencies, non-governmental organizations, international organizations, or international business.
