# Japan-Global Development Program (JGDP) — GSIR

## Overview

The Japan-Global Development Program (JGDP) is a **two-year master's program** offered jointly by the **GSIR and GSIM** to provide students the opportunity to learn universal development and growth logic in the fields of politics, economy, and management, using Japan's experiences in economic development and corporate growth as basic case materials.

The JGDP is not intended as a program to train Japan specialists. The logic and lessons from Japan's experience — both successes and failures — from the 19th Century to date will help future leaders from abroad when they think about strategies for development in their home countries.

## Three Distinctive Aspects

### Non-Occidental Development Model
The JGDP offers a model of Japan's nation building in the 19th Century through its continuous development in the 20th Century and efforts toward sustainable growth in the 21st Century. Particular emphasis on Japan's approach, management style, and culture contrasted with European countries' cases.

### Practical Knowledge
Students benefit from skills and knowledge applicable to their respective futures based on Japan's past experience in policy making, economic development, and public and business management.

### Phases of Development
Japan's rapid development from the Meiji Restoration in 1868 to now includes almost every phase of development, which students can relate to their home countries' states of development.

## JGDP Courses (GSIR Track)

- Modern Japan in the World
- International Relations and Foreign Policy of Japan
- Japanese Development Cooperation: Implementation and Practice
- Postwar Japanese Politics
- Japanese Government and Politics
- Japan's Education System
- Japanese International Development Cooperation
- Politico-economic History of Japan's Modernization

## JGDP Courses (GSIM Track)

- Agriculture Business and Policy in Japan
- Energy Policy and Business Leadership
- Japanese Style Management and Corporate Governance
- Japan's Major Industries and Human Resource Practices
- Monozukuri (Manufacturing) Management in Japan
- Small to Medium-sized Firms in Japan

Along with these JGDP courses, students are required to take courses based on their concentration requirements.

## Program Director's Message

**Gaku Funabashi, Professor and Program Director**

> "In the process of modernization over the past 150 years, Japan has experienced a variety of economic and social conditions and has gone through a process of trial and error in considering what is necessary for its development. Through JGDP, we hope that by linking Japan's experiences at different stages of development with knowledge in your own area of expertise, you will acquire the ability to think about strategies to take from situations you may encounter in the future."
