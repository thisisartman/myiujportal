# Public Management and Policy Analysis Program (PMPP)

**Ranked No. 5 in Asia — Eduniversal Best Master Ranking 2025**

## Two MA Tracks with Analytical and Managerial Training

The PMPP offers two **2-year master's degrees**:
1. **MA in Public Management (PM)**
2. **MA in Public Policy (PP)**

Students must complete coursework (**at least 40 credits**) and write either a thesis or research report on managerial or policy issues.

## Core Curriculum

**Required Basic Courses (all students):**
- Introduction to Policy Analysis
- Public Policy Process
- Statistical Methods
- Econometrics
- Microeconomics I

**PM Track Additional Requirements:**
- Managing Public Organizations
- Public Administration
- Public Finance and Budgeting

**PP Track Additional Requirements:**
- Cost Benefit Analysis
- Introduction to Policy Modeling
- Policy Evaluation

## Three Distinctive Features

### 1. Interdisciplinary Nature
PMPP encourages interdisciplinary learning to equip students with theoretical, analytic, and problem-solving skills. Collaborates with IDP, IRP, and MBA programs to expose students to diverse social science perspectives.

### 2. Empirical and Quantitative Approach
Emphasis on mathematics, statistics, econometrics, policy modeling, and other quantitative methods to analyze empirical data. Helps students identify and solve various managerial and policy problems.

### 3. Multicultural Diversity
The majority of PMPP students are **government employees** from approximately **50 countries** across the globe. Students exchange experiences and develop new ideas encountering similar management and policy problems in different institutional settings.

## Message from the Program Director

**Jiwon Jung, Associate Professor and Program Director**

> "PMPP is a global graduate program for future leaders in the public sector and ranked at No.5 in Asia by the Eduniversal Best Master Ranking 2025. We have three distinctive features. Second, PMPP enables students to take interdisciplinary approaches to public management and policy. Lastly, our greatest strength lies in the unique and diverse community.
