# GSIR — Our Features / Why GSIR?

## Global Recognition

**Earn a degree that is recognized and valued worldwide.**

GSIR's degree programs are internationally recognized:
- PMPP and IDP are ranked **No. 5 in Asia** in the **Eduniversal Best Masters Ranking 2025**
- IUJ is one of the **four selected partnership-track universities in Japan** by the IMF

## Learn in a Truly Global Environment

**Study alongside students and faculty from diverse cultural and academic backgrounds.**

The greatest strength of GSIR is the diversity of both its student body and faculty, as well as the wide range of course offerings suitable for both capacity building and advanced research. With **English as the medium of instruction**, our curricula meet international standards.

## Connect to the World

**Build strong academic and professional networks that extend beyond the classroom.**

GSIR has established academic links with renowned universities in North America, Europe, and Asia through exchange and linkage degree programs. It also maintains cooperation with Japanese and international organizations for career development.
