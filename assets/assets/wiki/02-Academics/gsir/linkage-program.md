# GSIR Linkage Program

The Linkage Program is a double-degree arrangement: students spend their first year at a partner institution and their second year at IUJ, earning degrees from both universities upon completion.

## Structure

- **Year 1:** Study at partner institution in the student's home country
- **Year 2:** Study at IUJ in Niigata, Japan
- **Outcome:** Two degrees — one from the partner university, one from IUJ (GSIR MA)

## Partner Institutions

### Indonesia
| Institution | Program |
|-------------|---------|
| Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) | International Relations / specific program |
| Universitas Indonesia (UI) | Relevant graduate program |
| Universitas Brawijaya (UB) | Relevant graduate program |
| Universitas Padjadjaran (UNPAD) | Relevant graduate program |

### Taiwan
| Institution | Program |
|-------------|---------|
| National Chengchi University (NCCU) | Graduate-level program in IR/Public Policy |

## Admissions

Linkage students apply through their home institution's linkage coordinator and separately to IUJ. Admission to IUJ is required for the Year 2 portion. For details, see the [Admissions – GSIR Linkage page](/academics/admissions/gsir-linkage/).
