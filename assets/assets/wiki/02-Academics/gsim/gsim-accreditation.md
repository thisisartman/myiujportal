# GSIM Accreditation & Recognition

## James Brian Quinn Scholar Award

Awarded to the **top 5% of GSIM graduates** based on cumulative GPA, beginning with the Class of 2019.

### About Dr. James Brian Quinn (1928–2012)

Dr. Quinn became the first Dean of GSIM in 1988 when the school opened with help from the Amos Tuck School of Business, Dartmouth College. He served as Dean for the first 4 years, working closely with IUJ Board Chairmen Sohei Nakayama, Jiro Ushio, and Yotaro Kobayashi.

**Career highlights:**
- Based at the Tuck School, Dartmouth; retired from professorship in 1993
- Degrees: BS (Yale), MBA (Harvard), PhD (Columbia); joined Tuck faculty in 1957
- International assignments in Geneva and Melbourne
- Advisor to many governments and U.S. Presidential administrations
- 1979: Participated in U.S. Department of Commerce mission to launch U.S. trade with the People's Republic of China
- 1989: Visited the Soviet Union as a National Academy of Science representative to advise the newly formed Gorbachev government on market economy strategies
- Chaired the Academic Committee for President Clinton's Domestic Policy Review on Innovation and Productivity

Published extensively on corporate and U.S. national policy issues; received numerous prestigious awards for teaching and articles in Harvard Business Review and other top management journals.

IUJ received permission from the Quinn family in October 2018 to name the award in his honor.

## Beta Gamma Sigma (BGS) Membership

[Beta Gamma Sigma](https://www.betagammasigma.org/home) is the international business honor society for students enrolled in **AACSB-accredited** schools (top 5% of business school programs worldwide).

- GSIM established the **BGS IUJ Chapter in 2018**
- Over **60 alumni** are BGS members (as of 2025)
- Awarded to the **top 20% of graduates** based on cumulative GPA

## James Brian Quinn Scholar Award

IUJ honors the top 5% of GSIM graduates (by cumulative GPA) with the **James Brian Quinn Scholar Award**, beginning with the Class of 2019. The award is named after Dr. J. Brian Quinn (1928–2012), who became the first Dean of GSIM in 1988 and served for four years, collaborating with IUJ Board Chairmen Sohei Nakayama, Jiro Ushio, and Yotaro Kobayashi. Dr. Quinn held degrees from Yale (BS), Harvard (MBA), and Columbia (PhD), and was a celebrated scholar in strategic planning and technological management.

## Beta Gamma Sigma (BGS) Membership

GSIM is accredited by AACSB (placing it in the top 5% of business programs worldwide), qualifying students for **Beta Gamma Sigma (BGS)** membership — the international business honor society. The IUJ BGS Chapter was established in 2018. Membership is awarded to the top 20% of graduates by cumulative GPA. Over 60 alumni had joined as of 2025.
