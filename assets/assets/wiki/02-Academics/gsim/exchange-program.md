# Exchange Program Opportunities (GSIM)

GSIM students can study abroad for one academic term at one of 30+ prestigious partner institutions across Asia, Europe, North America, and Africa. The program is designed to deepen global business understanding, expose students to diverse teaching methods, and build an international professional network.

---

## How It Works

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Timing** | Typically Fall term (September–December) of Year 2; Winter term (January–March) may be possible depending on partner availability |
| **Duration** | One term (3–4 months) |
| **Selection** | Competitive placement process |
| **Tuition** | Paid to IUJ (not the host school) |
| **Host school fees** | Waived under exchange agreements |
| **Travel/accommodation/meals** | Covered by the student |

**Note:** Exchange opportunities vary year to year. Not all partner schools accept students every year.

---

## Eligibility

- **MBA Program students** have priority in the selection process.
- **One-year program students** may apply after completing their degree in August, provided they continue enrollment and pay IUJ tuition for the Fall term.
