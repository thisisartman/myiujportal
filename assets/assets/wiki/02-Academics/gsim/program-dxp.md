# Digital Transformation Program (DXP)

**Duration:** 1 year | **Focus:** Technology leadership and digital transformation

## Overview

The DXP prepares future managers with strong capabilities in the technology domain. The program envisions graduates as "orchestra conductors" of DX initiatives — harmonizing technology, business strategy, and organizational change.

Key capabilities developed:
- Leverage emerging technology trends and design effective technical blueprints
- Address key business dimensions: strategy, marketing, people, organizational environment
- Communicate confidently with leaders across all functional areas
- Align diverse stakeholders around a shared DX vision

**Career paths:** Business leader driving digital initiatives, IT specialist advising on technology strategy, or entrepreneur launching innovative technology ventures.

## Program Structure

### Core
Essential, fundamental knowledge to understand and systematically analyze digital transformation subject matter.

### Elective
Wide range of choices tailored to career development needs and interests.

### Capstone
Deepens knowledge on specific, timely topics and issues.
