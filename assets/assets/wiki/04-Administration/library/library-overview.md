## Notes for Students

- Your **My Library** account shows all current loans and due dates
- **Off-campus access** is available for e-resources using your IUJ credentials
- Course reserve materials for your classes are organized by school (GSIR / GSIM)
- For books not in the collection, submit a **Purchase Request** or use **Inter-Library Loan**
