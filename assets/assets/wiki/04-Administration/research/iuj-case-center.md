# IUJ Case Center (ICC)

Established on **October 16, 2015** by the Graduate School of International Management (GSIM).

## Mission

To contribute to management education through high-quality research, development, and publication of case studies on organizations, especially in the Asian context.

## Objective

Sound managerial decision-making requires integrating knowledge across functional areas. Case studies and discussions provide an excellent pedagogical method for this. IUJ leverages partnerships with industry and institutional partners — including 54 sponsoring corporations within the Global Partnership Program — to develop case materials for academic and practitioner communities.

## Activities

- Facilitate case study development through academia-industry interaction
- Publish case studies developed by or in collaboration with the center's members
- Promote interdisciplinary approaches in management education
- Promote case method teaching across educational institutions in Asia Pacific
- Develop supplementary teaching materials alongside case studies
