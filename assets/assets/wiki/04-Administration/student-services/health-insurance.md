# Health Insurance

All IUJ students are enrolled in three insurance programs upon arrival. OSS handles enrollment and most payments automatically.

---

## 1. National Health Insurance (NHI)

All regular students are required to enroll in Japan's **National Health Insurance (NHI)**. This is a public health insurance scheme that covers 70% of eligible medical costs incurred within Japan.

- OSS arranges payment via your Japanese bank account
- Coverage is **Japan-only** — NHI does not apply to medical issues that arise outside Japan
- If you plan to travel internationally, purchase separate travel insurance before departure

---

## 2. Liability and Injury Insurance ("Gakken Sai")

All students are also required to enroll in this supplementary insurance, formally called **Gakken Sai** (学研災).

**What it covers:**
- Injuries you cause to other people or damage to property during IUJ-approved activities
- Injuries sustained by you personally during IUJ-organized events

**Premiums (subject to change):**

| Duration | Premium |
|----------|---------|
| 1 year   | ¥1,540  |
| 2 years  | ¥2,430  |
| 3 years  | ¥3,620  |

---

## 3. Scholarship Insurance

Students on certain scholarships may have additional insurance coverage arranged through their scholarship sponsor. Check directly with your scholarship office or sponsor organization to confirm what is included.
