# Support for Students with Disabilities

As part of its social mission, IUJ complies with relevant laws and regulations to eliminate discrimination based on disability and to realize a society where all citizens live together with mutual respect.

IUJ welcomes applicants with special needs due to disabilities to inquire about campus facilities and possible special arrangements for joining academic programs and participating in dorm life.

## Contact

- **OSS (general support):** oss@iuj.ac.jp
- **GSIR Admissions (admission screening):** admgsir@iuj.ac.jp
- **GSIM Admissions (admission screening):** admgsim@iuj.ac.jp

See also: [FAQ — Disability Support](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/faq/general/#toggle-id-16)
