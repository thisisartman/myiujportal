# Student Body Overview

The IUJ campus is comprised of approximately **375 graduate students from over 55 countries and territories** around the world. English is the shared language, used in classrooms and throughout campus life.

Students live together in campus dormitories and interact through sports groups, cultural clubs, and social activities — forming a true global community. Classroom discussions are rich and wide-ranging given the diversity, and conversations often spill over into dorm lounges and kitchens.

While a majority of students are from the greater Asian region (Fiji to Uzbekistan), students from Africa, the Americas, and Europe are also well represented on campus. A breakdown of current full-time students (excluding exchange and special students) is available as a [Student Count PDF](http://iuj.ac.jp/oss/studentbody/StudentCount.pdf).

## Alumni Community

As students graduate and return to their home countries or stay in Japan for employment, IUJ actively supports the growth of friendships and professional networks through Alumni Activities. IUJ alumni come from **over 130 countries** and maintain groups on LinkedIn and Facebook, as well as an official IUJ-maintained online alumni directory (alumni access only).
