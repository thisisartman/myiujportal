# Visiting Hospitals

For a full hospital guide including maps and contacts, see the [Guide to Health Care (2025)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/hospitalguide.pdf).

---

## English-Speaking Doctors for Internal Symptoms

If you have a cold, headache, high blood pressure, elevated blood sugar, or similar internal symptoms, go to one of these two clinics:

### Yukiguni Yamato Hospital
**English-speaking doctor:** Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday

### Moegi Clinic
**English-speaking doctor:**
- Monday, Wednesday, Friday (morning)
- Tuesday (morning)
- Thursday (afternoon)

---

## Important Rules When Visiting

- **Arrive on time** — late arrivals cause delays for everyone
- **Do NOT cancel without notice** — contact the Health & Wellness Office at Ext. 170 before cancelling or rescheduling
- **Do not leave the hospital without informing a staff member**
- **Expect long waits** — plan your schedule accordingly
- **Bring a translation app** (e.g., Google Translate) for smoother communication

---

## Contact

If you need help arranging a hospital visit or have health concerns:

- **Health & Wellness Office:** Ext. 170 | healness@iuj.ac.jp
- **Location:** A-Wing Building, 1st Floor (behind the ATM)
