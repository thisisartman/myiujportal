# MLIC Library

The Minakami Library and Information Center (MLIC) is IUJ's on-campus library, supporting academic research and providing access to international journals, books, and databases.

---

## Services

### My Library (User Record)
Check your current loans, reservations, and borrowing history: [My Library Portal](https://library.iuj.ac.jp/intrasite/CARINUSER.HTM)

### Reference Services
Librarians provide research support, source recommendations, and database navigation assistance. [Reference Services](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/library/services/reference/)

### Inter-Library Loan (ILL)
If a book or article is not available in MLIC's collection, you can request it from another library through the ILL service. [Inter-Library Loan](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/library/services/loaner/)

### Book Purchase Request
Students and faculty can request the library to purchase new books for the collection. [Book Purchase Request](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/library/services/orders/)

### Member Services
Information on borrowing limits, card registration, and extended access. [Member Services](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/library/services/members/)

### Library Guide
A full guide to library layout, policies, and resources: [Library Guide (PDF)](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/mlic/information/guide.pdf)

---

## Library Access

Library access is linked to your IUJ student account. Register your email and library account before arrival using the [library registration form](https://goo.gl/forms/A4Vz6ehE7G) shared during the September onboarding process.

For your active loans and due dates, see the **My Library** section on your Profile page in the MyIUJ! Portal, or visit the My Library portal directly.
