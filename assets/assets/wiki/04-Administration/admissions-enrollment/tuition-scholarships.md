# Tuition & Scholarships

## Tuition and Fees

Budget estimates per academic year for a single student (without family). All amounts in Japanese yen.

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Admissions Fee | ¥300,000 (one-time, first year only) |
| Tuition — 2-year program | ¥3,200,000 / academic year |
| Tuition — 1-year program | ¥3,900,000 / academic year |
| Dormitory fees | ¥384,000–468,000 / year (¥32,000–39,000 × 12 months) |
| Dormitory Maintenance fee | ¥10,000 (at move-in) |
| Utilities | ¥60,000 / year (¥5,000 × 12 months) |
| Meals | ¥360,000 / year (¥30,000 × 12 months) |
| Books & Academic Supplies | GSIR: ¥30,000 / year; GSIM: ¥90,000 / year |
| GSO-EC Student Fees | ¥4,500 |
| Special Program Insurance & Medical Assistance | ¥5,000 |
| National Health Insurance | ¥30,500 |
| Social & Entertainment | ¥120,000 / year (¥10,000 × 12 months) |

### Estimated First Year Total

| Program | Estimated Cost |
|---------|---------------|
| GSIR — 2-year | ¥4,504,000 – ¥4,588,000 |
| GSIM — 2-year | ¥4,564,000 – ¥4,648,000 |
| GSIR — 1-year | ¥5,204,000 – ¥5,288,000 |
| GSIM — 1-year | ¥5,264,000 – ¥5,348,000 |

> Notes: The ¥300,000 admissions fee is not required in the second year. Estimates assume students remain at IUJ during the summer. Food, utilities, and personal expenses vary by lifestyle. Travel expenses are not included.

For families, see the [IUJ Family Guide](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/living/FamilyGuide.pdf) which includes a real student family budget (2 adults + 1 small child).

## MEXT Scholarship — Embassy Recommendation

Applications begin ~18 months before enrollment:

1. Contact Japanese embassy in your country (~18 months before enrollment) for guidelines and forms
2. Preliminary screening at embassy (May–August, 19–13 months before)
3. Embassy recommends successful candidates to MEXT (early September, 1 year before)
4. Contact IUJ Admissions after passing embassy screening: GSIR: admgsir@iuj.ac.jp / GSIM: admgsim@iuj.ac.jp
5. Obtain Letter of Acceptance from IUJ (by August 10th, 13 months before enrollment)
6. MEXT makes final scholarship decision

**Eligible Programs:**
- Master's: All GSIR programs except IPPP; all GSIM programs
- PhD: All clusters

## Part-time Work

### On Campus

Starting from Spring Term Year 1. Hourly wage: ¥1,050. International students may work up to 28 hours/week with Immigration Agency permission. Positions are limited.

- Library staff (work permit required)
- Computer help desk staff (work permit required)
- Facilitators for non-degree programs
- Teaching Assistant
- Research Assistant

### Off Campus

Very limited for students without Japanese language proficiency. Work permit required. Some scholarships prohibit off-campus employment.

### Spouse Work

Dependent visa is for accompanying a student, not primarily for employment. Spouses may work up to 28 hours/week with a work permit. Childcare responsibilities must not interfere with the student's academic obligations. Spouses must reside at the same address as the IUJ student.
