## English Test Exemption

Applicants are exempt from submitting TOEFL/IELTS/TOEIC scores if they fall into one of these categories:

**Category 1:** Received an English-medium education for at least 4 years during secondary studies in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK, or the USA.

**Category 2:** Completed (or will complete) an undergraduate or graduate degree at an English-medium institution — must submit evidence that the medium of instruction is English (transcript alone is insufficient unless it explicitly states this).

## GSIM: GMAT/GRE or IUJ Math Test

GSIM applicants must submit an official GMAT or GRE score, OR take the IUJ Math Test (if eligible).

- International applicants outside Japan applying to programs **other than DXP**: IUJ Math Test available **only in the 3rd intake**
- DXP applicants and applicants residing in Japan: IUJ Math Test available in **any intake**
- For full scholarship consideration: strongly advised to submit GMAT/GRE by the 2nd deadline

### IUJ Math Test Details

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Duration | 1 hour |
| Questions | 12 (multiple-choice) |
| Format | Online |
| Calculators | Not permitted |
| Penalty | None for wrong answers |

**Topics covered:** functions, graphs & geometry; basic calculus (differentiation, integration, max/min); sets, probability & elementary statistics; miscellaneous word problems.

Level is equivalent to GMAT Quant. Based on Japanese high school math curriculum.
