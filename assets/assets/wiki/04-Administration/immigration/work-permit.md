# Work Permit (Shikaku-gai Katsu-do)

Students in Japan on a Student visa are expected to engage only in study and research. Any paid work — part-time jobs, internships, etc. — requires a **Work Permit** from the Immigration Office obtained **before** starting work.

## Exceptions (No Permit Needed)

- Teaching Assistant (TA) or Research Assistant (RA) positions on the IUJ campus
- Note: **Library jobs at IUJ do require a Work Permit**

## Working Hours Allowed (With Permit)

- **During term:** up to **28 hours/week**
- **School holidays** (e.g., summer internships): up to **40 hours/week**

## How to Get a Work Permit

**Option 1 — At the airport on arrival:**
Fill out the application form before landing; submit at immigration counters at major airports.

**Option 2 — After arriving at IUJ:**
Contact OSS. [Application Form (XLSX)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/living/work-permit.xlsx)

**Required documents:**
1. Passport
2. [Application Form](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/16-8.html?hl=en)
3. Residence Card
4. Student ID Card

## Important

- IUJ is required to report your work activities to the Immigration Office — notify OSS of any changes to your work situation
- Working without a permit is a criminal offence and can result in **deportation**; employers who knowingly hire students without permits also face penalties
- Use common sense: consider whether the job is appropriate for a graduate student

Contact OSS at oss@iuj.ac.jp for questions.
