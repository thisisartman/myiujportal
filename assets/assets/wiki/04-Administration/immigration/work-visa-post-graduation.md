# Work Visa (Post-Graduation)

## When to Start

Begin changing your visa status from Student to Working **as soon as you sign an employment contract** — ideally before graduation. You cannot start without a signed contract. The process takes **at least 1 month**.

**Critical:** Your student visa becomes technically invalid at graduation. You cannot receive a salary until a working visa is stamped in your passport. IUJ cannot assist with this process after graduation.

More guidance: [Career Services – After the Search](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/career/aftersearch/)

## Documents You Prepare

1. CV in Japanese (Rirekisho) showing all Japan stay dates clearly — [download blank form](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/career/pdf/RirekishoBlank.doc)
2. Proof of address in Japan (housing contract, or temporary company dormitory address)
3. Certificate of Graduation (diploma, English + Japanese) — or "Certificate of Prospective Graduation" if not yet graduated
4. Transcript (request from IR/IM Office; allow 2 days; get multiple copies before leaving campus)
5. Copy of passport (bring original)
6. Alien Registration Card (address must be current)
7. 2 photos (4cm × 3cm)

## Documents Your Company Prepares

1. Application for Change of Status of Residence
2. Signed Job Offer Letter or Contract
3. Letter of Reason (Japanese) explaining why they are hiring you
4. Copy of company legal registration
5. Company brochure
6. Company financial statement

## Where to Apply

Apply at the Immigration Office **nearest your company** (saves time for follow-up questions). Tokyo is faster than Niigata. If applying before graduation, you may still use your IUJ Yamato-machi address.

## Key Warnings

- Once application papers are accepted by Immigration, you may stay in Japan even if your student visa expires — but **you cannot leave Japan** until the work visa is stamped
- Do not leave Japan after graduation without a re-entry permit or valid work visa — you risk being denied re-entry
- After graduation, IUJ is no longer your guarantor and **cannot help** with immigration complications
- Changing address before picking up your visa? Notify the Immigration Office — you must collect from the office where you applied unless you request a transfer
