# Sightseeing & Dining Near IUJ

The Minami Uonuma / Niigata area has a rich mix of outdoor activities, cultural festivals, world-class ski resorts, and excellent food. Most spots are reachable by IUJ bus, bike, or short train ride.

---

## Local Highlights

### Outdoor & Nature

- **Botan-Yama** — small mountain directly behind campus; great for hiking
- **Osaki Dam Park** (3 km from campus) — feed colorful Koi, see Koi No Bori (carp kites) during Golden Week, cherry blossoms in spring; Hakkaisan Shrine and soba restaurant nearby
- **Hakkaisan Sake Brewery / Uonuma no Sato** (9 km from campus) — brewery tour, sake sampling, bakery, restaurants; many students bike there

### Ski Resorts

World-class ski resorts are located between campus and Tokyo, including **Naeba** (host of alpine events). IUJ's annual Ski Day is held at Hakkaisan Roku. Ski and snowboard season runs December through March.

- [Niigata Ski Resorts directory](https://www.snowjapan.com/japan-ski-resorts/prefecture/niigata)

### Cultural Festivals & Events

| Event | Timing | Details |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Firewalking Festival** | October 20 | Hakkaisan Shrine; famous nationally |
| **Naked Man's Festival** | First Saturday of March | Famous traditional festival |
| **Ojiya Balloon Festival** | 3rd weekend of February | Hot air balloons over snow fields; rides available |
| **Ojiya Bull Fights** | May–November, select weekends | Traditional sumo-style bull competition |
| **Ojiya Fireworks Display** | September 8–9 | Includes Guinness World Record launch |
| **Echigo Tsumari Art Event** | Every 3 years (Aug–Sep) | Major international art triennial |
| **International Festival of Rice** | Autumn | Celebrates Uonuma's famous Koshihikari rice |

### Where to Stay (for Guests)

- **Hotel Okabe** — nearest western-style hotel
- **Hotel Paramount** in Urasa — near the station
- **Cycling Terminal** at Hakkaisan Roku
- **Tejimaya** — simple Japanese inn (recommended)
- **Ryugon** in Muikamachi — beautiful traditional ryokan
- AirBnB options available (search Minami Uonuma City)

## Local Rice: Uonuma Koshihikari

Minami Uonuma is the home of **Koshihikari** — considered Japan's finest rice. **Majidon** is a local rice bowl specialty (English info: majidon.jp/en).
