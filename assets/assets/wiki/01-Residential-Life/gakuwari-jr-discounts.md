# Gakuwari: JR Student Travel Discounts

Gakuwari is a JR (Japan Railways) program that gives full-time students a **20% discount** on JR train fares for trips over 100 km one way. The discount applies to the base fare only — not express (shinkansen) surcharges.

**Full PDF guide:** [Gakuwari Guide (IUJ OSS)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/gakuwariguide.pdf)

---

## Eligibility

- Full-time IUJ students aged 12 and over
- **Exchange students and special students do NOT qualify**
- Trip must be over 100 km one way

---

## Coupon Allocation

- **10 coupons** per academic year (September 1 – August 31)
- Each coupon is valid for **up to 3 months** from the date of issue
- After your graduation date, coupons are only valid through that same calendar month

---

## How to Use

1. **Fill in the coupon at home** (the day before purchase is recommended):
   - Write the departure station and destination station in the fields provided (alphabet is fine)
   - If there's a transfer station, write that too
   - Mark the trip type: one-way (片道) or round-trip (往復) with a black or blue ink pen

2. The coupon **must be stamped by IUJ** (OSS) before it can be redeemed — it's invalid without the school's official stamp

3. **Purchase at Urasa Station** using the self-service ticket machine. A call center staff member will assist through the machine (only one person at a time can use Gakuwari at the machine). Expect this to take several minutes.
   - [YouTube video: How to use the machine at Urasa Station](https://youtu.be/DEEBb9mZdkE)
   - Go early or the day before to avoid missing your departure

4. **Present your Student ID** at purchase and whenever requested during travel

---

## Rules

- Only the named student may use the coupon — not family members or friends
- Coupon is valid only while your student status is active
- Do not alter, modify, or tamper with any printed fields — the coupon becomes invalid and such action is strictly forbidden
- Coupons must be redeemed before the trip starts (not after boarding)
