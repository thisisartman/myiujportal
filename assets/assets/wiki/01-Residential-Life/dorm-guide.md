# Dorm & Facility Guide

## Residential Policy

IUJ operates a **mandatory residential policy** — all students live on campus through graduation. This is intentional: the 24/7 immersive environment is central to cross-cultural communication and diversity training. Exceptions are made only when IUJ cannot provide appropriate housing (e.g., for families) or under special conditions.

---

## Single Dorms

IUJ provides **300 private rooms** for single students across three single dorms (SD1, SD2, SD3).

**Each room includes:**
- Private bathroom
- Bed and bedding
- Desk
- Phone

Additional furnishings and decorations are the student's responsibility.

**Room Assignment Process:**
- Incoming students are guaranteed a room for their first year — no request needed
- IUJ invites students to submit room preferences
- Room assignments are communicated by email in **late August**

**Shared Dorm Facilities:**
- Large lounges for group gatherings (one with billiard table and large-screen TV; one with CNN/BBC TV and video player)
- Small lounges for study or relaxation
- Shared kitchen per dorm (students have dry goods storage, pots/pans space, and shared refrigerator)
- Students must clean kitchens after use

---

## Married Student Apartments (MSA)

**18 units** available for officially married couples. Units are unfurnished two-room apartments with a kitchenette and separate bedroom.

**Eligibility:**
- Officially married couples only (not for unmarried partners)
- Not intended for families with children — a limited number of units per year may be allocated to couples with non-school-aged children (up to 2 children under 5)
- IUJ may give priority to couples where both partners are IUJ students, or to special needs situations

**Lottery System:**
- **May lottery** — for current 1st-year students to move in the following October
- **First week of October lottery** — for newly arriving students to move in early November

IUJ recommends waiting until Spring of the first year before bringing family to campus.

## Cafeteria (Shokudo)

| Meal | Hours | Last Order |
|------|-------|------------|
| Lunch | 11:30 – 13:30 | 13:15 |
| Dinner | 17:30 – 19:00 | 18:45 |

**Days:** Monday – Saturday (closed Sundays)

**Payment:** Cash or PayPay

**Dietary options:**
- 95% of selections are Halal
- Vegetarian options always available
- Daily set menu or à la carte selections

Notable items: hand-made yogurt, takeout pizza. Weekly menu available on the @iuj page.

**Snack Lounge:** Vending machines on 2F of the cafeteria building.

---

## IUJ School Shop

One-stop shop for daily essentials on campus.

**Hours:**
- Monday – Friday: 8:00 – 19:00
- Saturday & Sunday: 13:00 – 19:00
- Closed during Year-End/New Year holidays

**Available items and services:**
- Drinks, snacks, hot coffee (Halal options available)
- Sandwiches, Onigiri (rice balls), obento
- Cleaning and school supplies
- IUJ original merchandise
- Dry cleaning service
- Postal and parcel delivery services
- Business card printing services

## Gymnasium & Sports Facilities

- **Full-sized gymnasium** — popular in evenings for basketball, volleyball, soccer, badminton, and more
- **Workout room** with exercise machines
- **4 outdoor tennis courts** (2 are lit for night play)
- Balls and nets provided; personal equipment (rackets, shuttles, etc.) must be supplied by students
- Jogging, biking, hiking, and walking paths immediately available from campus
