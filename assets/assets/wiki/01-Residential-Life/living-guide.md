# Living Guide

IUJ is located in Minami Uonuma, Niigata Prefecture — a region known for its rich natural environment and four distinct seasons.

## Climate in Minami Uonuma

| Season | Conditions |
|--------|-----------|
| Spring | Mild |
| Summer | Hot and humid |
| Autumn | Mild |
| Winter | Cold with heavy snow |

Winters offer excellent conditions for skiing and snowboarding at nearby resorts.

## Living in Urasa

Urasa is a safe and convenient town for student life. Nearby amenities include supermarkets, drugstores, hospitals, and restaurants. A supermarket near Urasa Station also sells halal food.

## Access to Tokyo

Urasa Station is on the **Joetsu Shinkansen** line, providing direct access to Tokyo in approximately **90 minutes**.
