# Off-Campus Housing

## IUJ Residential Policy

IUJ has a **mandatory residential policy**: all students are expected to live on campus through graduation. The policy exists to develop soft skills and foster a strong professional network in an international environment.

- **Single students** live in on-campus dormitories (SD1, SD2, etc.), or in IUJ-facilitated apartments approximately 1.5 km from campus if dorm capacity is exceeded
- **Married students** may be eligible for on-campus Married Student Apartments (MSA) — accommodating 2 adults and up to 2 children under 5 years of age

## When Off-Campus Living May Apply

Off-campus housing may become necessary **only if IUJ cannot accommodate family members** on campus. In such cases:

- There are some older apartments along the IUJ Bus Route, but a car is usually required
- Initial move-in costs are typically equivalent to **3 months rent**
- Monthly rent ranges from approximately **¥40,000–¥70,000**, plus utilities, community fees, and transportation
- OSS can introduce you to English-speaking landlords and real estate agents with units on the IUJ bus route

**Academic requirement:** Students living off-campus must reside within the near vicinity — on the IUJ Bus Route or within a **20-minute commute**. Living further away requires special permissions from the Dean.

> Note: IUJ shuttle bus runs from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM only. Late-night commutes after 8 PM must be arranged by the student independently.

## Moving Off Campus

Students wishing to move off campus with family members must obtain:

1. Prior approval from the Dean
2. Likely permission from your scholarship host organization

This is considered a private decision; IUJ's support resources in this area are limited.

## Bringing a Spouse and Children

### Dependent's Visa Requirements

Family members who are not Japanese nationals **must obtain a Dependent's Visa** before coming to Japan.

> **Warning:** Family members should NOT come on a tourist visa unless they plan to stay under 90 days. Extensions and status changes are not possible on tourist visas.

The IUJ student serves as the guarantor and must apply for a **Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)** for the dependent visa. This process:

- Can take **up to 4 months**
- Is not guaranteed to succeed
- Requires demonstrating sufficient income to support the family (most scholarships may not qualify)

> JICA students: consult with JICA separately regarding family visa support.

### How to Apply for the CoE

1. Establish your own residency in Japan first
2. Demonstrate sufficient income to immigration officials
3. Submit the relevant forms to the Immigration Office

For guidance, contact the Japanese Embassy or Consulate in your home country, or your country's Embassy in Tokyo. OSS can provide the forms but **cannot manage the process on your behalf** as it is a private matter.

### Timing Recommendation

It is strongly advised to wait until **Spring Term of your 1st year** before inviting family — or ideally **Fall of your 2nd year** — for the following reasons:

1. The first two terms at IUJ are academically intensive
2. Heavy snowfall can make adjustment to Japan more difficult in winter
3. For school-aged children, Japan's school year begins in April — starting mid-year creates disruption; confirm school capacity with the city office beforehand

### Spouse Employment

Dependent visa holders come for the purpose of supporting the household and children's schooling — **not to work outside the home**. Children under 3 should remain at home, as local daycare is a welfare service reserved for specific circumstances.
