# Car Registration at IUJ

Everyone associated with IUJ who owns a car or motorcycle and parks it at any time on campus must inform the OSS of their car registration number. You must have an IUJ original car sticker to use the parking lots — without exception.

When your car is no longer needed (e.g., at graduation), go to any dealer to have it disposed of, or transfer ownership legally with the help of a car dealer. The OSS can provide assistance.

## IUJ Car Parking Permit (Shako-shomei)

To receive your parking permit, submit the following to the Office of Student Services:

**Copies of:**
- Your valid driver's license — either a Japanese license, or an international license plus your home country's license
- Your Shaken-sho (car registration papers)
- Your Third-party Insurance papers (Nin-i Hoken)
- ¥1,000 for issuance of the parking permit

## Parking Areas

- **Dormitory residents** should use the space near SD2 or SD3
- **Off-campus residents** should use the space above the Baiten or near the Library — do **not** use the Main Entrance parking area
- The area nearest the Main Entrance is reserved for short-term campus visitors and must **not** be used by students, staff, or faculty during business hours

## Note on International Driving License Renewal

When renewing your international driving license in your home country, you must stay there for **at least three continuous months** by law. Likewise, if you have lived in Japan and want to use a new International License here, you must have been out of Japan for a **full 3 months**. Your home country must also be listed in the [Geneva Convention](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss/carinfo/geneva.cfm).
