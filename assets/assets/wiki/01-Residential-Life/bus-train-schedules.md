## Shinkansen (Bullet Train)

The nearest Shinkansen station is **Urasa** on the Joetsu Shinkansen line.

- **[Urasa ↔ Tokyo / Niigata Shinkansen Schedule (PDF)](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/travel/shinkansen.pdf)** — updated March 2025
- **[Shinkansen + Local Train Connections for Joetsu Lines (PDF)](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/travel/shink_To_Local.pdf)** — updated March 2025

## Bikes

All bikes parked on campus must be **registered with the IUJ Dorm Director**. Go to SD1 to get a sticker (with your name) for your bike. Review and follow [Japan's bike riding laws](https://www.npa.go.jp/koutsuu/kikaku/trafficsafety/traffic_safety_english.pdf).
