# Campus Food Services

## IUJ Cafeteria (Shokudo)

The IUJ cafeteria serves lunch and dinner Monday–Saturday.

- **Lunch:** 11:30–13:30
- **Dinner:** 17:30–19:00
- **Payment:** Cash or PayPay

**Halal:** Approximately 90–95% of all cafeteria dishes are Halal-certified. The IUJ Muslim Student Association (MSA) has reviewed and approved these practices. Chefs with over 10 years of Halal experience use:
- Ingredients with certified Halal labels, cross-checked against approved/prohibited ingredient lists
- Non-alcohol-based cleaning products
- Dedicated Halal knives and cutting boards
- Appropriate oils, with strict separation of Halal and non-Halal products during cooking

The weekly cafeteria menu is available on the [@iuj student information page](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/atiuj/menu/).

## IUJ School Shop

The on-campus convenience shop carries snacks, drinks, instant miso soup, and daily essentials.

- **Weekdays:** 8:00–19:00
- **Weekends:** 13:00–19:00

**Halal items available:** In-house baked breads, freshly brewed coffee, drinks, and handmade yogurt (available in 4 varieties) are all Halal.
