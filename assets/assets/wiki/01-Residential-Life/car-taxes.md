# Car Taxes in Japan

All car owners pay an annual tax on the vehicles they own, in addition to taxes paid at the time of purchase. Taxes are paid to the Transport Authority Office every fiscal year. Tax notices arrive by mail around the dates below.

## Annual Tax Deadlines

| Vehicle Type | Notice Arrives | Payment Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Sedan (regular car) | Around May 10 | May 31 |
| Light vehicle (kei car) | Around April 19 | April 30 |

## Selling or Disposing of Your Car

If you sell or dispose of your car **without** correctly notifying the Transport Authority, you will continue to be charged annual taxes. You remain responsible until the appropriate forms are filed.

For assistance, contact:
- **JA Autopal at the Nokyo:** 025-772-8811
- Any licensed car dealer

Cars that are abandoned are traced to their owner. That owner remains responsible for the car and all associated taxes — IUJ may be asked to contact a graduating student to obtain payment. **Do not abandon your car.**

## Tax Transfer Examples

**Selling to a third party or trading in:**
The original owner is responsible for the full annual tax in the year the car was purchased. The new owner is charged starting from the next April 1 fiscal year. Negotiate with the buyer about reimbursement for taxes already paid.

**Disposing of your car (scrapping):**
The annual tax is recalculated monthly from the date of disposal registration. You may apply for a refund for months between disposal and the following April. If you do not register the disposal, you are liable for the full annual tax every year.

**Stolen car:**
Contact the Tax Office. Muikamachi Tax Office: **025-773-6668**

## Summary

- Always file the correct ownership transfer forms when selling — use the Nokyo (025-772-8811) or a licensed dealer
- Always notify the tax office when disposing of a car, and confirm the dealer processes the paperwork in your presence
- Take care of all car-related paperwork before graduation — contact the OSS if you need help
