# Traffic Accidents, Offenses & Laws

Steep monetary penalties are imposed for even minor traffic offenses in Japan. Speeding, parking violations, and minor accidents can easily cost over ¥40,000. Beyond fines, there is a point system that can suspend (6–14 points) or revoke (25+ points) your license.

- Running a red light: 2 points
- Speeding: up to 12 points
- Any alcohol in your system: points skyrocket

## IUJ Policy

**Drunk driving is not tolerated by Japan, and certainly not by IUJ.** Those involved will risk losing their scholarship, suspension, or expulsion — or both. Those who allow a friend to drive drunk are held responsible as well.

Traffic violations involving IUJ students are most often reported by the police directly to the OSS. IUJ will be involved in communications with students, especially when insurance claims are involved — this is standard procedure by local police, not IUJ's choice.

> If you are involved in any traffic incident, accident, or violation, **inform OSS before the police do** so we can prepare for communications with police and insurance companies.

## Daiko Service (Designated Driver Taxi)

There is no need to drink and drive. The Muikamachi area has a service called **Daiko** — a special taxi that brings you, your friends, and your car back to campus for just slightly more than a regular taxi fare. Use it.
