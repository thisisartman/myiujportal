# Dorm Guidance

Welcome to the IUJ Dormitory! All newly arriving students are housed on campus in accordance with IUJ's Residential Policy.

## Key Documents

Download these official guides from OSS:

- [Dorm Guide (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/dorminfo.pdf) — rules, facilities, and living information
- [Dorm Pledge (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/dormpledge.pdf) — agreement all residents must sign
- [Residential Policy (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/ResPolicy.pdf) — as of September 1, 2022
- [Temporary Away Notification Form (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/TemporaryAway.pdf) — required if leaving campus for an extended period
- [Off Campus Move Request Form (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/Off-CampusReq.pdf) — for students needing to move off campus
- [Application for Exceptions Review Committee (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/ResPolicyExceptionRequestForm.pdf) — for requesting exceptions to residential policy
