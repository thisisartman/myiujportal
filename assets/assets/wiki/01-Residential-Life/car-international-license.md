# International Driver's License: Geneva Convention Countries

Only countries listed in the **Convention on Road Traffic (Geneva)** may use an International Driver's License in Japan, combined with their valid home country license. If your country is not listed here, you **cannot** drive in Japan without a Japanese license.

**Note:** China, Indonesia, Mongolia, Vietnam, and many other countries are NOT on this list.

**Critical rule:** Even if your country IS listed, if you have been in Japan for any length of time, a new or renewed International License is only valid if you have been **out of Japan for a FULL 3 months**, proven by passport stamps. Even one day short means you are driving illegally.

## Geneva Convention Member Countries (recognized for Japan)

Albania, Algeria, Argentina, Australia, Austria, Barbados, Belgium, Benin, Botswana, Bulgaria, Cambodia, Canada, Central Africa, Chile, Congo, Cote D'Ivory, Cuba, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Dominica, Ecuador, Egypt, Fiji, Finland, France, Georgia, Ghana, Greece, Guatemala, Haiti, Hong Kong, Hungary, Iceland, India, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Jamaica, Jordan, Korea, Kyrgyz, Laos, Lebanon, Lesotho, Luxembourg, Macao, Madagascar, Malawi, Malaysia, Mali, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Morocco, Namibia, Netherlands, New Zealand, Niger, Norway, Papua New Guinea, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Rwanda, San Marino, Senegal, Serbia, Sierra Leone, Singapore, Slovakia, South Africa, Spain, Sri Lanka, Sweden, Syria, Thailand, Togo, Trinidad and Tobago, Tunisia, Turkey, UAE, Uganda, UK, USA, Vatican, Venezuela, Yugoslavia, Zaire, Zimbabwe

**Provisionally accepted (former Soviet states):** Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Belarus, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Moldova, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Croatia, Macedonia, Slovenia

**Special cases:** Swiss, German, and French national license holders may drive in Japan for one year after arrival with an official Japanese translation attached to their license (no International License needed).

For more information: [NIA Niigata International Exchange Foundation](http://www.niigata-ia.or.jp/)
