# Getting to IUJ — Access & Directions

## Campus Address

**777 Kokusai-cho, Minami Uonuma-shi, Niigata 949-7277 JAPAN**

IUJ is located approximately 230 km northwest of Tokyo, in the Echigo-sanzan mountain valley near the town of Urasa in Niigata Prefecture.

---

## By Bullet Train (Joetsu Shinkansen) — Recommended

Take the **Toki SuperExpress** to **Urasa Station**:
- From **Tokyo Station**: approximately **90 minutes**
- From **Niigata Station**: approximately **40 minutes**

At Urasa Station:
1. Exit through the **ticket gate** (there is only one).
2. Turn **left** toward the **East Exit**.
3. Go down the **second stairwell on the left**.
4. You will find the **IUJ shuttle bus stop**.

### IUJ Shuttle Bus
- Operates **Monday–Friday**, approximately **8 am – 8 pm** (once per hour)
- Special schedule on Saturdays and Sundays
- Journey time: approximately **10 minutes** from Urasa Station (East Exit) to campus
- [Full bus timetable (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/iuj_bus.pdf)

---

## By Car (Kan'etsu Expressway)

Take either the **Muikamachi Exit** or **Koide Exit**, then follow **Route 17** to Urasa Station and IUJ:
- From **Tokyo**: approximately **3.5 hours**
- From **Niigata City**: approximately **1.5 hours**

Alternatively, use the **Yamato PA Exit** (ETC system only) — approximately **10 minutes** from campus.

---

## Climate & Environment

The campus sits in a mountain valley surrounded by the **Echigo-sanzan** peaks. The area offers:
- **Autumn:** Pleasant and clear; 10–15°C (50–60°F)
- **Winter:** Around freezing with heavy snow accumulation
- **Spring:** Sunny and variable; 15–21°C (60–70°F)

Outdoor activities available year-round: mountain climbing, hiking, skiing, hot springs.
