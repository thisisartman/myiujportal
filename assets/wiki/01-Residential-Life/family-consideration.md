# Family Consideration

Students considering bringing family members to Japan during their studies should read the [IUJ Family Guide](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/FamilyGuide.pdf) carefully before making a decision.

> Note: A recommended timeline applies to international students (does not apply to Japanese nationals or JISR students).

## Recommended Timing

The first two academic terms at IUJ are very demanding. Students are strongly encouraged to settle into IUJ life, experience the winter term, and build friendships and professional networks **before** bringing family members. The recommended timeline below supports academic success and a positive family experience.

### Timeline

| Period | Recommended Actions |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Fall Term Year 1 — September** | Arrive at IUJ; move into single student dorm; settle into campus life |
| **November** | Contact city office about childcare and school availability; submit family health documents to OSS or JICA Joint Project Office (JJPO) |
| **December** | Prepare documents and apply for Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) at Immigration (see Info Pack for required documents) |
| **Spring Term Year 1 — January** | Contact landlords and arrange housing starting from mid-March |
| **January – February** | Coordinate moving plans with OSS; submit "Request to Move Out" form at least 4 weeks before moving (around mid-February) |
| **Late March – Early April** | Family arrives in Japan; family stays for approximately one year (one school year for children) |
| **Summer Break & Fall/Winter Term Year 2** | Enjoy Japan with family; note that elementary school summer holidays are in August |
| **Spring Term Year 2 — April – June** | Family prepares to return home; children may need to return home or be homeschooled — consult local school |
| **After Graduation** | Student and family return to home country together |
