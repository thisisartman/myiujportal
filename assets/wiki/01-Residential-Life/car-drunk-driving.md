# Drunk Driving & Seat Belt Policy

## Drunk Driving

**Drunk driving is not tolerated by Japan, and certainly not by IUJ.**

Those involved in drinking and driving incidents risk losing their scholarship, being suspended or expelled from IUJ — or both. Those who allow a friend to drive drunk are also held responsible.

### Penalties

If your breath test shows an alcohol content of **0.15 mg/L or more** (lowered from the previous threshold of 0.25 mg/L), you face:
- **Fine:** ¥100,000 to ¥500,000
- **Imprisonment:** 6 months to 5 years

## Use Daiko — There's No Excuse

The Muikamachi area has a service called **Daiko** — a designated driver taxi that brings you, your friends, and your car back to campus for just slightly more than a standard taxi fare. There is absolutely no need to drink and drive.

**DON'T DRINK AND DRIVE.**

## Seat Belts

As of June 1, 2008, **all passengers** in both front and rear seats are required by law to wear seat belts at all times while the vehicle is in motion.
