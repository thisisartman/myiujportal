# September Arrival Guide (Incoming Students)

This page consolidates everything new students need to do before and upon arriving at IUJ in September. Read through all sections carefully and complete each action item on time.

> **Note:** The arrival schedule is subject to change. Always watch for OSS updates, especially closer to September.

## Action Items (Complete Before Arrival)

### 1. Certificate of Eligibility (Student Visa)
OSS provides step-by-step instructions for filling out the required forms. This must be done before you can enter Japan as a student.

### 2. Dorm Room Preferences
All incoming students are assigned a single dorm room. Room address is communicated by **early September** (or early July for summer students).

- Submit room preferences **as early as possible** — deadline is typically **late July**
- Your assigned room will generally remain your Japan address until graduation
- Arrival dates vary by group and are strictly enforced due to dorm maintenance schedules

### 3. IUJ Email / Library Registration
Fill out the [library registration form](https://goo.gl/forms/A4Vz6ehE7G) before arrival so your `@iuj.ac.jp` email is active when you land. Emergency contact information is also collected through this form.

### 4. Facebook Support Group
Join the [IUJ Support Facebook Group](http://www.facebook.com/groups/IUJSupport2025/) using the name you used on your application and your Application ID. A great way to connect with incoming classmates before arrival.

### 5. Japanese Language Intensive Program (IJP)
The CLEAR office offers a 4-day intensive Japanese program in mid-September for students with no Japanese background. A fee applies. Sign up before you arrive — see [Intensive Japanese Program](../02-Academics/general-procedures/intensive-japanese-program.md) for details.

### 6. Mentor Program
Each incoming student is paired with a 2nd-year student Mentor. Check the OSS website in August for your Mentor's name and email. Your Mentor will contact you between early September and early October.

## On Arrival

- Attend **Orientation** as scheduled (refer to the September Arrival Schedule PDF above)
- Check in to your assigned dorm room
- Review the [Dormitory Rules and Guidance (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/dorminfo.pdf)
- **Register your bike** with the Dorm Director at SD1 if you bring one
