# Dorms and Facilities

IUJ provides on-campus housing where students live and study together in an international environment. Dorm life offers 24/7 opportunities for cross-cultural communication and lasting friendships.

## Student Housing

IUJ provides three Single Dormitories (SD1, SD2, SD3) and a Married Student Apartment (MSA).

- **SD1, SD2, SD3** — for students living alone
- **MSA** — for married students/students with families; details in the [IUJ Info Pack](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/InfoPack.pdf)

All rooms are fully furnished from day one.

## Single Dormitory — In-Room Facilities

Each room includes:

- Desk & Chair
- Bed with storage drawers (100 cm × 210 cm) — mattress, cover, and sheets provided; changed weekly at no cost
- Private bathroom (bathtub, shower, shower curtain, sink, toilet)
- Closet
- Air conditioner
- Window blinds
- Telephone
- LAN (internet) connection
- TV cable outlet (TV not provided)

## Shared Facilities

Residents of single dormitories have access to:

- Lounges
- Kitchens (students must bring their own cooking utensils and kitchenware)
- Refrigerators
- TVs
- Washing machines and dryers
- Vacuum cleaners
- Storage area for winter tires

Provides daily necessities, food, and IUJ original goods. Also offers postal services, parcel delivery, and dry cleaning.

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|

## Gymnasium and Sports Facilities

- Full-sized gymnasium (basketball, volleyball, soccer, badminton)
- Workout room with exercise machines
- Four outdoor tennis courts (two equipped with lighting for night play)
