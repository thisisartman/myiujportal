# Express Bus (Highway Bus)

## Overview

A highway bus runs between Muikamachi and Tokyo (Ikebukuro or Shibuya) regularly. Schedules are available via [Japan Bus Online](https://japanbusonline.com/CourseSearch/11100040001). For other highway bus destinations, see the [Japan Bus Online top page](https://japanbusonline.com/).

---

## Key Details

| Item | Info |
|---|---|
| Route | Muikamachi ↔ Ikebukuro (Tokyo) / Shibuya |
| Frequency | Every hour between 10am and 3am |
| Travel time | ~5 hours 20 minutes |
| Fares | Under ¥3,000 (varies by time of day and season) |
| Gakuwari | **Not applicable** on highway buses |
| Reservations | Required — book and pay at any **7-Eleven** convenience store |
| Refund policy | Non-refundable |

---

## Getting to the Bus Stop

The nearest stop to IUJ is in **Muikamachi**, near the AEON complex, on the highway itself. Private transport to the stop is required — walking from Muikamachi Station is possible but approximately 4 km.

---

## Booking

Reservations must be made at a 7-Eleven. Payment is collected at time of booking.
