# Purchasing a Car

All cars parked at IUJ (day-time or long-term parking) must be registered with the OSS. All students, staff, and faculty who drive must obtain an IUJ parking sticker — without exception.

Responsibilities and procedures for owning and operating a car in Japan are likely different from your home country. Read through all sections below carefully before deciding to purchase a car.

> **Drunk driving warning:** Drunk driving is not tolerated by Japan, and certainly not by IUJ. Those involved in drinking and driving incidents run the risk of losing their scholarship, being suspended or expelled — or both. The legal implications are also severe. Those who allow a friend to drive drunk are held responsible as well.
