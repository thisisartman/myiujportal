# Driving in Japan: License Requirements

**Your home country license alone is not sufficient to drive legally in Japan.** You must also have a valid International Driver's License bearing the text "Geneva Convention 1949 September 19th." If your country is not on the Geneva Convention list, you must obtain a Japanese license.

If you have already driven in Japan for one year on an international license, or have lived in Japan for any length of time, you must obtain a Japanese license to continue driving legally — **unless** you have been out of Japan for a full 3 months (proven by passport stamps).

- [Japan traffic rules and regulations (NPA)](https://www.npa.go.jp/english/bureau/traffic/)
- [JAF: Traffic rules in Japan](https://english.jaf.or.jp/safe-driving/traffic-rules-in-japan)

## Key Rules to Know

- STOP sign: a red upside-down triangle reading 止まれ
- At all railroad crossings: come to a full stop, roll down the window, and listen for trains before proceeding
- International license valid for **one year only**; renewal requires proof of 3+ months in your home country

## When You Need a Japanese License

You must obtain a Japanese license if any of the following apply:
- Your country's international license is not recognized in Japan
- Your license or international license will expire during your stay
- You have already driven in Japan for one year on an international license
- You have lived in Japan, left for less than 3 months, and returned wanting to drive

## How to Get a Japanese License (Conversion)

**Required documents:**
- Valid home country license + photocopy
- Certified Japanese translation (JAF: 025-284-7656, fee applies)
- Juminhyo (住民票) — residence certificate from City Hall (not your Residence Card)
- Passport showing at least 3 months in your home country during the license's validity period
- Two recent photos (3 cm x 2.5 cm)
- International Driver's License recognized by Japan
- Japanese-speaking friend or interpreter (OSS cannot provide translation for licensing purposes)
- Reservation at the License Center in Niigata

**Step 1 — Make an appointment (in Japanese):**
聖籠町運転免許センター (Seiroumachi Unten Menkyo Center)
Tel: **025-256-1212**

Confirm documents to bring: passport, international license, residence card, Juminhyo, etc. Also confirm the fee.

**Step 2 — Getting to the Niigata License Center:**
- Shinkansen: IUJ → Urasa → Niigata Station → Bandai Bus Terminal → express bus to Seiroumachi Menkyo Center
- Local train: IUJ → Urasa → Nagaoka → bus to Niigata → bus to Seiroumachi
- By car: IUJ → Koide IC → Niigata Nishi IC → By-Pass heading North → follow signs to 聖籠町 / 運転免許センター

**Step 3 — After the initial interview:**
Follow-up visits are typically at:
長岡運転免許試験場 (Nagaoka Unten Menkyo Shikenjou)
Tel: **0258-22-1050**

Getting to Nagaoka Center:
- Train: IUJ → Urasa → Miyauchi Station → taxi/walk to Nagaoka Menkyo Shikenjou
- By car: IUJ → Route 17 toward Nagaoka (~1 hr) → near JR Echigo-Takiya Station, take the flat road left → follow signs

**Step 4 — Tests:**
Written exam, driving test, eye examination (chart reading + color blindness test). Passing on the first or second attempt is uncommon — be patient.

## Notes

- **Translation services:** Contact your Embassy in Tokyo, or JAF (03-6833-9100 or 025-284-7656). OSS cannot provide official translation — IUJ translations are not accepted by the police at license centers.
- **Expired home country license:** If your license expires, you cannot do a conversion — you must take the full Japanese license process (courses + exams in Japanese, expensive).
- **Swiss, German, or French license holders:** May drive for one year in Japan with an official Japanese translation attached. No international license needed.
