# Student Organizations & Campus Clubs

In early October each year, incoming students are introduced to all active clubs at an orientation session and are encouraged to sign up with that year's club leader. Students can also start new clubs at any time.

---

## Student Representative & Governance Groups

These groups are elected positions that shape campus life and academic representation:

**GSO Executive Committee**
Graduate Student Organization. Coordinates campus social activities and overall club coordination across all students.

**IR Council** *(GSIR)*
Handles academic matters and organizes academic events such as debates and speech contests.

**MBA Council** *(GSIM)*
Handles academic matters and organizes events such as business plan competitions.

**HOUSE Program**
A year-long friendly competition among the **10 HOUSES of Nakayama** (named after Nakayama Hall). Each house has 30–35 students.
- **Head of House** (elected): Coordinates year-long competitions and scoring
- **HOUSE Captains**: Lead their house in various activities competing for the HOUSE Championship

## Starting a New Club

Don't see your favorite activity? It is easy at IUJ to create a new club — find like-minded students and submit a proposal to OSS. See [Starting a New Club at IUJ](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/future-students/campus-clubs-and-events/new-club/) for the process.
