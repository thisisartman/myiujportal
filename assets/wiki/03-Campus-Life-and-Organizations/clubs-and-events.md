# Clubs and Events

Campus life at IUJ is active and student-driven. Graduate students lead events, clubs, and activities, creating a vibrant campus environment. Students are encouraged to participate, take initiative, and make their IUJ experience their own.

## Club Activity

A wide range of clubs and activities is available across sports, culture, arts, and professional interest areas. Students are also welcome to start new clubs.

- [Full List: Campus Clubs and Activities](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/campus_clubs_events/club_activities/)

## Annual Events

Throughout the year, the campus hosts a variety of student-led events:

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **International Festival** | Students share their cultures through food and performances |
| **Cultural Festival** | Highlights traditions from around the world |
| **Ski Day** | Students enjoy skiing and snowboarding in Niigata's snowy mountains |

These events create opportunities to connect across cultures and build lasting friendships beyond the classroom.
