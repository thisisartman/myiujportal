# Alumni Voices

## Overview

The Alumni Voices section features written comments, videos, and career stories from IUJ graduates around the world, organized across five career showcases.

---

## Sections

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| Alumni Comments | Written reflections on the IUJ experience (renewed 2024) |
| Alumni Videos | Direct video messages from alumni |
| Career Stories | Individual alumni career paths post-IUJ |
| Showcases | Alumni featured in 5 categories (see below) |
| IUJ Ambassadors | Students who volunteer to speak with prospective applicants |

---

## Alumni Showcases (5 Categories)

- Business Professionals
- Entrepreneurs
- Government Officials
- Academics and Researchers
- International Organizations

---

## Talk to an Alum

Current students and prospective applicants can request to be matched with an **IUJ Ambassador** — a volunteer alumnus who answers questions about campus life and career outcomes.
