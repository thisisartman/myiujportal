# On-Campus Events

IUJ campus life is shaped by a rich calendar of student-organized events throughout the academic year. Events change as student cohorts change — this list is a representative sample, not exhaustive.

For events happening around the Minami Uonuma area, see [Off-Campus Events](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/future-students/campus-clubs-and-events/off-campus-events/).

---

## Fall Term (September–December)

- New student arrivals and orientations (academic and campus life)
- Flea Market
- Welcome Party
- Career Counseling workshops and one-on-one coaching sessions
- Resume Book preparation
- GSO-EC Fall elections
- IR Council and IM Council elections
- Halloween Party
- **Around the World @ IUJ** (mid-November) — festival for local townspeople featuring country games, handicrafts, dance, traditional dress, and music from IUJ's diverse student body
- Sports and club meetings throughout term
- Dean's Hours

---

## Winter Term (January–March)

- **IUJ Ski Day** — group ski trip to local resorts
- IUJ Snow Sculpting Festival (not held every year)
- International Snowball Fight in Koide
- Chinese New Year celebrations
- Japanese Language Program "Happyokai" (performance event)
- Valentine's Day Party (organized by GSO-EC)
- **IUJ Unplugged** — acoustic music concert by IUJ musicians
- 4-on-4 Football/Soccer Tournament (gym)
- Billiards Tournament
- Happy Hours and Dean's Hours
- Sports and club meetings throughout term

---

## Spring Term (April–June)

- **IUJ Open Day / International Festival** — IUJ's flagship event open to townspeople; features food from around the world and a multi-stage show of IUJ student talent
- GSO-EC Spring Elections
- IR/IM Spring Council Elections
- **IUJ Olympics** — fun inter-dorm and off-campus resident sports competition
- Billiards Tournament
- Graduation Party
- Happy Hours and Dean's Hours

---

## Summer

- Various events organized around the IEP (Intensive English Program) summer session
