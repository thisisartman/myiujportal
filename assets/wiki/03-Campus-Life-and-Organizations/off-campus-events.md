# Off-Campus Events & Activities

IUJ's location in Niigata Prefecture offers students rich seasonal experiences throughout the year, from local festivals and outdoor adventures to cultural events across the region.

---

## Fall (September – December)

### Festivals & Cultural Events
- **Katakai Fireworks** — September 9–10; one of Japan's largest fireworks festivals
- **Hakkaisan Fire Walking Festival** — October 20; a traditional fire ritual at Hakkai-san
- **Ojiya Bull Fights** — Traditional bull sumo (ushi no tsunatori); held through mid-November

### Nature & Outdoors
- **Fall Leaf Viewing (Koyo)**
  - Okutadami Lake area
  - Saguri Dam
  - Hakkai-san gondola ride
- **Onsen visits** — Hot spring baths throughout the region; especially enjoyable in cooler weather
- **Botan-yama Climb** — Scenic mountain hike near campus

### Day Trips
- **Teradomari Seaside** — Coastal town with fresh seafood and an aquarium
- **Joetsu Outlet Mall** — Shopping destination accessible by car or bus

---

## Winter (January – March)

### Snow Sports & Festivals
- **Skiing** — OSS arranges discounted lift tickets; several ski resorts accessible from campus
- **Koide Snowball Fights** — Community snowball fight event in Koide town
- **Muikamachi Snow Festival** — Local winter festival in Muikamachi
- **Tokamachi Snow Festival** — Features one of the world's largest snow stages; a regional highlight
- **Haddaka Oshiai Matsuri (Naked Man's Festival)** — Held on the first Saturday of March; a traditional endurance festival

### Campus & Vicinity
- **Snow Shoeing** — On and around the IUJ campus grounds
- **Rotenburo (Outdoor Hot Springs)** — Open-air onsen experiences; ideal during heavy snowfall

---

## Spring (April – June)

### Cherry Blossom Viewing (Hanami)
- IUJ Nature Park (on campus)
- Koide Ski Grounds
- Osaki Dam area

### Outdoor Activities
- **Osaki Dam Giant Fish Feeding** — Feed enormous carp and freshwater fish at the dam
- **Kite Flying in Sanjo** — Traditional kite flying event
- **Rice Planting Festival** — Participate in traditional rice paddy planting
- **Mizunashi River Biking** — Cycling along the river trail
- **BBQ Along Uono River** — Outdoor barbecue spots by the river
- **Fishing** — Obtain a local fishing license and fish in area rivers and streams
- **Miyanoya Soba Shop** — Highly recommended; try the Sansai Tempura soba

---

## Summer (July – August)

### Festivals
- **Fireworks Festivals** — Held throughout the region all summer; multiple towns host displays
- **Yamato Street Dancing** — Traditional Bon dance festival in Yamato town
- **Ojiya Bull Fights** — Summer dates in addition to the fall season
- **Watermelon Festival at Nokyo** — Late July to early August; local agricultural cooperative festival

### Unique Experiences
- **Hand-Made River Boat Launching in Yuzawa** — Build and launch boats on the river
- **Firefly Viewing in Muikamachi** — Visit "Hotaru no Sato" (Firefly Village) to see fireflies in the wild

---

## Tips

- OSS organises group trips and discounts for several of these events — check the OSS bulletin board or www2 portal for announcements
- Many venues are accessible by car; students with valid licenses are encouraged to carpool
- For bus/train access to regional destinations, see the [Bus & Train Schedules](../01-Residential-Life/bus-train-schedules.md) page
