# GSIM Features — Why GSIM?

## International Curriculum

English-only international curriculum covering essential business skills and rigorous training. Topics extend well beyond traditional business subjects, including:
- Data analytics
- AI technology and digital transformation
- Social business
- Energy transition
- Smart city
- Sustainability

## Multicultural Residential Campus

Faculty and students from diverse cultural backgrounds live together and interact, fostering cross-cultural understanding and rich professional development.

## Japan Contents

The curriculum offers **14 courses** specializing in Japan's economics, industry, business, leadership, and development history. Japanese language training courses are available at no extra charge, giving international students deep insight into Japan.

## Generous Scholarship

More than **80% of students** receive full or partial scholarships. The school offers generous scholarships to qualified applicants despite program tuition costs.

## Partnerships with Top Institutions

Strong ties with:
- Top Japanese companies
- Government organizations
- Local companies in the Niigata area
- Many top business schools worldwide (exchange programs)

## Research Seminar

Each student is assigned to a faculty member in an area of interest for one-on-one or small-group training while conducting their own research project. Submitting a strong research proposal is an important part of the application process.

## IUJ Case Center (ICC)

Established October 16, 2015. The ICC fosters engagement between students and practitioners through:
- On-site company visits
- Workshops
- Case method pedagogy (primary teaching approach)

Mission: develop socially-responsible global business leaders with ethical integrity and professionalism.
