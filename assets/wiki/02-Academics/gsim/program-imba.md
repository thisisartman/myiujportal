# Intensive MBA Program (IMBA)

**Duration:** 12 months (1 year) | **Track:** Accelerated master's

## Overview

The IMBA prepares future business leaders to meet challenges of climate change, digitalization, AI, ESG issues, and emerging market dynamics. The 12-month curriculum is compact, rigorous, and demanding, using case-based learning focused on both advanced economies and emerging markets in Asia, Africa, and Latin America.

Students from over 60 countries work, play, and learn together on campus. Alumni gain access to IUJ's global alumni network.

## Program Structure

### Core
Essential, fundamental knowledge and skills: understanding and analyzing business components systematically for real-world application.

### Elective
Wide range of choices tailored to career development needs and interests.

### Capstone
Summer Capstone courses (final wrap-up): practical knowledge in leadership, strategic thinking, and ESG issues awareness.
