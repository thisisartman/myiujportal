# International Social Entrepreneurship Program (ISEP)

**Eligibility:** JOCV ex-participants or candidates / International JICA scholars only
**Degree awarded:** Master of Science in Social Entrepreneurship (MSE)

## Overview

ISEP provides opportunities for young people who wish to:
- Work as social entrepreneurs and contribute to solving sustainable social issues through business and NPOs
- Work for social development in partnership with the private sector at international organizations and aid organizations

In addition to on-campus lectures, the curriculum incorporates a **trial business plan** simulating actual social entrepreneur activities through **field study in developing countries or rural areas of Japan**.

Students can also earn an **International Organization Professional Focus certificate** alongside their MSE degree, catering to those aiming for roles in international development/cooperation with the UN, JICA, and similar organizations.

## Program Structure

### Core
Essential fundamental knowledge of business administration including the latest trends in social businesses, to analyze and structure one's future business for solving social issues systematically.

### Elective
Wide range of choices from both business administration and international relations perspectives, covering how to start businesses and understanding factors causing social issues.

### Field Study
Minimum **4 months** of field study — builds local networks to realize the social business plan, and verifies/clarifies areas for improvement by prototyping products and services as solutions to social issues.

Credit requirements: [PDF](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/2526_creditrequirements_isep.pdf)

## Cross-school Curriculum

ISEP students study business administration as future social entrepreneurs while being able to take nearly half of courses from **GSIR**, gaining deeper understanding of different specialization fields and world regions — enabling them to view social issues from multiple perspectives.
