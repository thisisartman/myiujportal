# GSIM Linkage Program

**Outcome:** Two degrees — one from IUJ (GSIM), one from a partner university

## Overview

The GSIM Linkage Program allows students from partner universities to earn two academic degrees upon successful completion of both institutions' graduation requirements. The double-degree program is based on existing curricula of both IUJ and the partner institution.

- **Year 1:** Study at the partner university
- **Year 2:** Study at IUJ (GSIM)

## Partner Institutions

| Institution | Country | Program(s) |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Universitas Gadjah Mada | Indonesia | Master of Regional and Urban Planning (Faculty of Economics and Business); Master of Development Economics (Faculty of Economics and Business) |
| Diponegoro University | Indonesia | Master of Regional and Urban Planning (Faculty of Economics and Business) |
| National University of Mongolia | Mongolia | MBA Program |
| Beijing Normal University, Business School | China | MBA Program |

## How to Apply

1. Contact your home university to receive a nomination
2. After receiving a nomination, submit your application at: [/admissions/im_linkage/](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/admissions/im_linkage/)
