# Message from the Dean — GSIM

**Dr. Hyunkoo Lee**
Dean and Professor, Graduate School of International Management

## Opening opportunities for you…

Studying business in a graduate school is an effective way to upgrade your career. You can gain useful skills and improve your job qualification. You can enter a new industry or switch your career, even in a different country. Probably, the best part of a study in a graduate school is to get to know many unique people whom you usually don't encounter often. You will be amazed by their diverse talent and enthusiasm. Together, you can build a strong network for life.

GSIM offers various two- and one-year master's degree programs with a different emphasis. All programs transform students into all-round managers who can succeed in tomorrow's digital world. GSIM offers a delicate blend of both Western and Japanese management approaches. Domestic students often use programs as a springboard to an international post. International students use them as a stepping stone to a job in Japan.

Students receive a hands-on learning experience through:
- Rigorous core courses
- Small group seminars
- An integrated research project
- A wide range of electives
- International exchange programs
- Internships and exciting events

The multicultural campus provides unequaled opportunities to build a global network and discover international careers. Generous scholarships are available for qualified applicants.

The world is changing fast with transforming digital technologies, unconventional geo-policies, and new business opportunities. There is a growing demand for a new breed of business leaders who have courage and skills to navigate the volatile world.
