# MBA Program

**Duration:** 2 years | **Accreditation:** AACSB International

IUJ's MBA is the pioneer of U.S.-style business education in Japan, serving as a bridge between the international and Japanese business communities. Ranked among the best MBA programs in Japan by The Economist "Top 100 Business Schools" and listed in the top tier of Japanese business schools by the QS WUR ranking.

## Overview

The MBA curriculum equips students with knowledge, skills, and mindset for global business with strong emphasis on:
- Sustainability
- Digital transformation
- Innovation and business analytics
- Ethical leadership and social responsibility

## Program Structure

### Year 1 — Core Foundations and Global Mindset

Strong foundation in essential management disciplines: finance, marketing, operations, management, and analytics. Skills developed through case studies, interactive workshops, and team-based projects.

### Year 2 — Specialization and Career Focus

Students customize learning through electives and specializations aligned with career goals, plus career preparation activities. Free Japanese language programs available for international students pursuing careers in Japan.

## Curriculum

**Core:** 360° perspective courses covering management fundamentals.

**Elective / Specializations (7 areas):**
1. Finance
2. Marketing
3. Strategy
4. Digital Technology and Operations
5. Sustainability
6. Data Analytics
7. Innovation and Entrepreneurship
