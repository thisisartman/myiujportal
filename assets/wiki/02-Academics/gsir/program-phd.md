# PhD Program — GSIR

## Research Clusters

The PhD program consists of **three research clusters** based on specialized research areas, developed from the three existing Master's programs in GSIR:

### International Relations Cluster
Fosters researchers with specialized knowledge and high-level research ability in International Relations (including security), and professionals who can apply this to international organizations and government bodies.

### Economics Cluster
Fosters researchers with specialized knowledge and high-level research ability in Economics (including quantitative and statistical methods), applicable to economic development or policy formulation roles.

### Public Management Cluster
Fosters researchers with specialized knowledge and high-level research ability in Public Management, applicable to effective management roles in international organizations and government bodies.

## Degree Requirements

PhD candidates must satisfy the following while enrolled for **a minimum of 3 years**:

1. **6 credits (3 courses)** from "Core Required Courses":
   - **IR Cluster:** Advanced Studies in Theories of International Relations, Advanced Studies in International Political Economy, Advanced Studies in International Security
   - **Economics Cluster:** Advanced Microeconomics, Advanced Macroeconomics, Advanced Econometrics
   - **Public Management Cluster:** Advanced Public Management, Advanced Politics and Governance, Advanced Public Policy Process
2. **9 credits** from PhD Dissertation Advanced Seminars I, II, and III (3 credits each)
3. **Pass the comprehensive examination** at end of 1st year (one retake allowed in 2nd year)
4. **Pass the final examination** of their PhD dissertation. The dissertation is comprised of **three academic papers**, one of which must be **accepted by a refereed academic journal**

## 3-Year Schedule

| Period | Key Activities |
|--------|---------------|
| 1st Year | Core Required Courses (6 credits) + Dissertation Seminar I; Supervisor selection; Comprehensive Exam |
| 2nd Year | Dissertation Seminar II; Research; Interim Report; Writing/posting academic papers |
| 3rd Year | Dissertation Seminar III; Paper acceptance; Dissertation submission; Final Exam; PhD degree conferred |

## Facilities for PhD Students

### Dormitories
IUJ dormitories offer students international experience. Three single student dormitories (SD1, SD2, SD3) and one married student dormitory (MSA). Each single room has private bathroom, Wi-Fi, LAN access, and is furnished. Common facilities include computer lounges, meeting rooms, common kitchens, washing machines, TV lounges with satellite, billiards room, tatami room, prayer room, and storage.

### Special Study Room (Exclusively for PhD Students)
Located on 1st floor of MLIC, equipped with lockers, desks, printer, cabinets and bookshelves. Close to PC rooms and library.

### Matsushita Library and Information Center (MLIC)
Extensive database subscriptions, thousands of journals and newspapers (many full text), world economic statistics, financial data and market research services. PC rooms with Windows systems, high-speed internet.
