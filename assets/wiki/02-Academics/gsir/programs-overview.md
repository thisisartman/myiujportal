# GSIR Programs Overview

## Programs Offered

### International Relations Program (IRP)

The International Relations Program (IRP) at IUJ provides a comprehensive, interdisciplinary understanding of major trends and current issues in contemporary international politics, economics, and society. Special emphasis is placed on developments in the Asia-Pacific area and on Japan in particular.

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/irp/)

### International Development Program (IDP)

The educational purpose of the International Development Program (IDP) is to train students as analysts, practitioners and administrators who have acquired a scientific way of looking at important issues in society.

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/idp/)

### Public Management and Policy Analysis Program (PMPP)

The Public Management and Policy Analysis Program (PMPP) at IUJ is a global graduate program for future leaders in the public sector. PMPP is ranked **No. 5 in Asia** by the Eduniversal Best Master Ranking 2025.

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/pmpp/)

### Japan-Global Development Program (JGDP)

Japan-Global Development Program (JGDP) is a two-year master's program to provide students the opportunity to learn universal development and growth logic in the fields of politics, economy and management, using Japan's experiences in economic development and corporate growth as basic case materials.

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/jgdp/)

### International Public Policy Program (IPPP)

The International Public Policy Program is a one-year mid-career program, designed for diplomats and government officials of countries in Asia, the Pacific, and around the world. It is a program designed in collaboration with the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA).

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/ippp/)

### PhD Program

The PhD program, according to specialized research areas, consists of 3 clusters that are formulated by further developing the 3 existing Master's programs in GSIR:
- International Development Program
- Public Management and Policy Analysis Program
- International Relations Program

[Read More](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/phd/)
