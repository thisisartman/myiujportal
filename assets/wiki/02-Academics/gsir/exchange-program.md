# Exchange Program Opportunities (GSIR)

GSIR students can study abroad for one academic term at one of 10+ prestigious partner institutions across Asia, Europe, and North America. The exchange is designed to broaden global perspectives, expose students to different teaching methods, and build international professional networks.

---

## How It Works

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Timing** | Typically the Fall term (September–December) of Year 2 |
| **Duration** | One term (3–4 months) |
| **Selection** | Competitive placement process |
| **Tuition** | Paid to IUJ (not the host school) |
| **Host school fees** | Waived under exchange agreements |
| **Travel/accommodation/meals** | Covered by the student |

**Note:** Exchange opportunities vary year to year. Not all partner schools accept students every year.

---

## Eligibility

- **Two-year program students** have priority in the selection process.
- **One-year program students** may apply to go on exchange after completing their degree in August, provided they continue enrollment and pay tuition for the Fall term.

## Exchange on Your Own Campus

Even without going abroad, IUJ welcomes exchange students from partner schools each year, creating opportunities for cross-cultural connection and networking right on campus.
