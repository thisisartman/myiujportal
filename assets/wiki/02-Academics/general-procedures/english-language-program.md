# English Language Program (ELP)

The English Language Program (ELP) is offered through CLEAR (Center for Language Education And Research). Since English is the sole official medium of communication at IUJ, all enrolled students are expected to have sufficient English proficiency upon entry. The ELP provides targeted academic writing and communication support on top of that baseline.

All ELP courses maintain a **maximum class size of 15 students** to keep sessions interactive and allow one-on-one tutorials.

## Course Descriptions

### Academic English I (Fall — Required)
A pre-intermediate course focused on reading and listening to short academic texts, discussing key information, and synthesizing it into short pieces of writing. Emphasises vocabulary accuracy, sentence structure, and paragraph coherence. For students who need to refresh foundational academic English skills for IUJ assignments and exams. **Required students only.**

### Academic English II (Fall / Winter — Required)
An intermediate course focused on writing short essays or reports in academic style. Covers summarising and synthesising academic sources, essay organisation (introduction, body, conclusion), and compare-contrast language. Required for students who took Academic English I in Fall; also available for required students in Fall directly.

### Academic English Literacy (Fall — Optional)
Develops core academic literacy skills: comprehending academic texts and lectures, building arguments, avoiding plagiarism, public speaking and debate, and writing well-organised academic reports. **Open only to students exempted from Academic English I.**

### English for Research Writing — Fall (Optional, 2nd Year)
A twice-weekly course for second-year GSIR/GSIM non-native English speakers. Ideal for students new to IUJ or needing additional academic English support before starting their thesis/research report. Covers academic writing fundamentals, organising a research paper, self-editing, plagiarism avoidance, and summarising from multiple sources.
*1 credit; 2 × 90-min classes/week; 2 individual tutorials/term.*

### English for Research Writing — Spring (Optional, 1st Year / 1-Year Program)
For first-year and 1-year program students who want to begin thesis work over the summer. Covers academic paragraphing, transitions, synthesising ideas, organising a research proposal/introduction, and paraphrasing.
*1 credit; 2 × 90-min classes/week; 2 individual tutorials/term.*

### English for Thesis Writing — Fall (Optional, 2nd Year / 1-Year)
For students who are ready to begin writing their thesis or research report in Fall. Covers writing the major parts of a research paper: introduction, methodology, results, and discussion.
*1 credit; 2 × 90-min classes/week; 2 individual tutorials/term.*

### English for Thesis Writing — Winter (Optional, 2nd Year)
A continuation course designed for students who took English for Research Writing in Fall. Focuses on applying academic writing style (clarity, accuracy, logic) directly to thesis chapters (e.g., literature review, methodology). All assignments are linked to the student's actual thesis draft.
*1 credit; 2 × 90-min classes/week; 2 individual tutorials/term.*

### English for Professional Communication — Spring (Optional, 1st and 2nd Year)
Develops professional spoken English skills for post-IUJ careers. Focus areas: socialising, interviewing, meeting management, and negotiations. An online information session is held in the second module of Winter term for interested students.
