# Intensive Japanese Program (IJP)

The Intensive Japanese Program (IJP) is a short, paid pre-semester program held in mid-September, just before the start of the Fall term. It is designed primarily for incoming students who have little or no prior Japanese language experience.

---

## Key Details

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Timing** | Mid-September (before Fall term begins) |
| **Duration** | 4 days |
| **Fee** | ¥10,000 (includes all class materials) |
| **Credits** | Non-credit (does not count toward degree requirements) |

---

## Who Should Attend

The IJP serves two groups of students:

1. **Non-continuers** — Students who do not plan to enrol in a regular Japanese course during the academic year but want basic survival Japanese for daily life in Urasa (shopping, healthcare, local communication).
2. **Prospective Japanese course students** — Students who plan to take Basic Japanese 1 or Elementary Japanese 1 and want to prepare for the qualifying test administered at the start of the Fall term.

---

## What You'll Learn

- Hiragana and katakana (Japanese phonetic scripts) — reading and writing
- Basic survival phrases for everyday situations
- Foundational vocabulary to support the qualifying test preparation

---

## Recommended App

CLEAR recommends using **Ganbatte Kana** (available on iOS and Android) to practice hiragana and katakana before and during the IJP. Familiarising yourself with both scripts before arriving will significantly accelerate your learning.

---

## Registration

Registration details are distributed to incoming students during the pre-arrival orientation period. Contact CLEAR directly for the current year's schedule and registration form.
