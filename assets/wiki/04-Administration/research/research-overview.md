# Research at IUJ

IUJ conducts research across international relations, international management, and development economics through several dedicated centers and institutes.

## Research Units

### Research Institute
The IUJ Research Institute conducts and supports academic research. It includes research fellows and offers grants and publication support.

- [Director's Message](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/about/message/)
- [History of the Research Institute](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/about/history/)
- [Research Fellows](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/about/fellows/)
- [Grants](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/grants/)
- [Publications](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/publication/)
- [Research Support](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/support/)
- [Archive](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/institute/archive/)
- [PhD Program](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/academics/gsir/programs/phd/)

### GLOCOM (Center for Global Communications)
An external research affiliate conducting research on information and communications in a global context.

- [GLOCOM Website](https://www.glocom.ac.jp/en/)

### IUJ Case Center (ICC)
Established October 2015 by GSIM. Develops and publishes business case studies for management education, with a focus on Asian business contexts and interdisciplinary approaches.

- [Case Center Details](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/research/icc/)
