# Visa Procedure

## Student Visa

To attend IUJ from overseas, a valid passport and a student visa are required.

- Passport must be valid for at least **6 months after arrival in Japan**. If not, renew or apply for a new one before coming.
- A **Student Visa** must be stamped in your passport before arriving in Japan, in accordance with Japanese Immigration Laws.
- IUJ recommends using the **proxy application** process, where IUJ supports you through the visa application.
