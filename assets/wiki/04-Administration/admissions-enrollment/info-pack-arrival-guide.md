# Info Pack — Pre-Arrival Guide

## Overview

The **IUJ Info Pack** is the comprehensive guide for incoming students covering how to prepare for arrival, getting to campus, settling in, and campus life in the surrounding area. It is intended as a reference throughout your entire stay at IUJ.

> No printed copies are distributed. Download the PDF or save the link for frequent reference.

**Updated:** Every year in **April** — always ensure you have the most current version.

---

## Download

The Info Pack PDF is available from the Arrival Guide page on www2.iuj.ac.jp.

---

## Family Guide

Students planning to bring family members should also download the **Family Guide**:

## What the Info Pack Covers

- Pre-arrival preparation checklist
- Getting to IUJ campus
- Settling into campus life
- Housing and dorms
- Health insurance enrollment
- Banking and utilities setup
- Local area guide
- Academic procedures

---

## Notes

- Full details on all three health insurance programs are included in the Info Pack
- Reviewed and expanded at Fall Orientation each September
- If you miss orientation, OSS staff can guide you through the pack
