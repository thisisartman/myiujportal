# Pre-Departure Health Checklist for New Students

Take care of these health items **before leaving your home country**. Japan's healthcare system and pharmacy stock differ significantly from most countries — preparation now saves time, money, and stress later.

## 1. Visit Your Doctor

- Schedule a general health check-up before departure
- Discuss any ongoing medical conditions and get advice for managing them abroad
- Bring **enough medication for your first few months** in Japan, plus written prescriptions
- Common medications to stock up on: headache/migraine relief, digestive aids, allergy/seasonal medications, any chronic condition treatments

## 2. See Your Dentist

Dental care in Japan can be expensive and involve long wait times.

- Get a complete dental check-up
- Professional cleaning
- Treat any existing issues (cavities, tooth pain) before you leave

## 3. Pack a Basic Health Kit

Some over-the-counter medicines common in your country **may require a prescription in Japan** or may not be available at all. Prices are also often higher.

**Suggested items:**
- Pain relievers (ibuprofen, paracetamol)
- Cold and flu medication
- Digestive aids (antacids, anti-diarrheal medicine)
- Emergency contraception (morning-after pill) if you think you might need it
- Antiseptic cream, itch relief, basic first-aid supplies

> **Important:** There are regulations on bringing medication into Japan. Review the official guidelines before packing:  
> [MHLW: Bringing medicines for personal use into Japan](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/english/policy/health-medical/pharmaceuticals/01.html)
