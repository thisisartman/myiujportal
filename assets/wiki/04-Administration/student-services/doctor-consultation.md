# Monthly Doctor Consultation Service

IUJ offers a **Monthly Doctor Consultation Service** held on the **third Tuesday of each month** to support students with health-related concerns.

## What the Service Provides

The consultation offers **guidance on health concerns and treatment options**. It is an advisory service only:

- **No diagnoses**
- **No physical examinations**
- **No prescriptions**

The goal is to help you understand your symptoms and decide on the appropriate next steps for care.

## Booking

- A booking form is sent **3 days before** each consultation date
- Only **7 time slots** available per month — availability is not guaranteed
- Book early; slots fill quickly

## What to Include When Booking

- Your specific symptoms (e.g., pain, discomfort, or other concerns)
- When the symptoms began
- The area(s) of your body affected
