# Alumni Resources

## Overview

The IUJ Alumni Relations hub provides resources for graduates including the alumni directory, newsletters, donation options, and community events.

---

## Resources

| Resource | Description |
|---|---|
| Alumni Directory | Login-gated — available to alumni, faculty, and staff |
| Alumni Newsletter | Most recent and archived editions of Alumni News |
| Alumni Voices | Videos and showcases featuring IUJ alumni |
| Donate to IUJ | Furusato Nozei (ふるさと納税), Thanks Fund, Give2Asia.org (USA) |
| By Alumni, For Alumni | Online speaker series organized by alumni |
| IUJ WorldWide Friday | Annual global alumni chapter event — September 11, 2026 |

---

## IUJ WorldWide Friday

**IUJ WorldWide Friday** is held annually on the Friday nearest September 11. Chapter leaders worldwide coordinate local events to renew the IUJ network. The 2026 date is **September 11, 2026**.

---

## New Registrants

Graduating students and new alumni can register their profile via the alumni portal to be included in the directory and receive newsletters.

---

## Contact

- Alumni inquiries: IUJ Alumni Relations Office (contact via www2.iuj.ac.jp/alumni/)
- Speaker series / events: Contact Alumni Relations directly
