# Health and Wellness Office

The Health and Wellness Office (HWO) supports the well-being of students, faculty, and staff at IUJ. Services include health consultations, first aid, health exams, referrals to external healthcare facilities, and information on infectious diseases and wellness.

**Location:** A-Wing Building, 1st Floor (behind the ATM)  
**Phone:** 025-779-1170 (Ext: 170)  
**Email:** healness@iuj.ac.jp

---

## Services

### Walk-in Health Consultations (Registered Nurse)

The Health and Wellness Coordinator (a Registered Nurse) is available for personalized health advice and wellness concerns:

- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–12:30 PM and 1:30 PM–5:00 PM
- No appointment needed — walk-ins welcome

### Monthly Doctor Consultations

A school doctor visits campus monthly for physical and mental health consultations. Announcements are posted approximately 1 week before each visit. Check the OSS calendar for upcoming dates.

### Annual Health Checkups

Mandatory under the School Health and Safety Act. Each student is eligible for one checkup per year:

- **When:** April and October
- **Recommended:** Fall term of your first year at IUJ
- You cannot do both April and October in the same year — choose one

---

## Important Policies

- IUJ **does not provide hospital escort services** as a general policy. Exceptions may be made in rare circumstances.
- IUJ is **not responsible** for managing healthcare or hospital visits for students' family members. Family healthcare logistics are the student's own responsibility.
