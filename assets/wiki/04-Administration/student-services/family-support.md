# Family Support Information

This guide supplements the official IUJ Family Guide, focusing on pregnancies, infants, childcare, and schooling. All figures are for foreign students covered by National Health Insurance (NHI). Check OSS for the current Family Guide.

---

## Pregnancy

**Where to go:** Kikan Hospital (~4 km from campus) has obstetrics/gynecology with modern facilities. The hospital in Koide is an alternative.

- Visit a hospital at the **first sign of pregnancy** — standard practice in Japan
- At 3–4 months, visit the Kenshin Center (Group Health Check-up Center) to register your pregnancy and receive:
  - **Mother and Child Health Handbook** (*Boshi Kenko Techo*) — carry to every appointment
  - **14 free check-up vouchers** (*Ninpu Ippan Kenshin Muryo Jyushin Hyo*) for routine prenatal visits
- Routine prenatal check-ups and healthy deliveries are **not covered by NHI** (pregnancy is not considered a medical condition)
- Medical complications during pregnancy may be partially covered
- Keep all receipts — some expenses may be reimbursed after registering your child's birth at the city office

**Free prenatal classes** are offered at the hospital's check-up center. Classes are in Japanese only — bring a Japanese-speaking companion.

## After the Baby is Born

### Registration Deadlines

| Action | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| Report birth at City Office | Within 14 days |
| Register child at City Office | Within 60 days |
| Apply for Dependent Visa (if staying >60 days) | Within 30 days of birth |

> **Important:** If your baby stays in Japan more than 60 days without the appropriate visa, they become undocumented. Apply for "Permission to Acquire Status of Residence" (Dependent's Visa) at the Immigration Office promptly.

### Childbirth Allowance (*Shussan Ikuji Ichijikin*)

- Normal delivery costs approximately **¥420,000**
- If covered by NHI, you can receive a **¥420,000 subsidy** from Minami Uonuma City
- Apply at the City Office when registering the birth
- Payment goes directly to the hospital or to your bank account

### Infant Health Check-ups (Free)

Check-ups are provided at ages: 1 month, 4 months, 7 months, 10 months, 12 months, 18 months, 24 months, 30 months, and 3 years.

- 7-month check-up: individual appointment at Kikan Hospital pediatrics
- All others: group check-ups at the Kenshin Center

### Vaccinations

Japan has a public immunization schedule. Key natural immunities fade after 3–8 months. Contact Kikan Hospital soon after arrival with your child's full immunization records to ensure they are up to date with Japanese requirements.

## Childcare: Day Nurseries and Kindergartens

- **Nursery:** Ages 0–6
- **Kindergarten:** Ages 3–6
- Enrollment is quota-based — spaces fill in **December** for the April start of Japan's academic year
- Children under 3 with a parent available at home may not qualify for KodomoEn (childcare center)
- Both parents must be full-time students, ill, or working full-time with no grandparent available for priority eligibility

> **Action required:** Confirm space availability at the city hall **before** family members arrive. Notify the school and city hall at least 1 month in advance if your child will leave or no longer need the service.

---

## Compulsory Education (School-Age Children)

Japan requires 9 years of schooling: 6 years elementary + 3 years junior high, starting at age 6.

- School year starts in **April**
- Enroll at the City Office, Education Division (*Kyoiku-iinkai*) — bring your child's Resident's Card
- Children on/near campus typically attend **Urasa Elementary School** or **Yamato Junior High School**
- Instruction is in Japanese; Urasa Elementary has limited English support
- No tuition fees, but parents pay for lunches, school bags, textbooks, trips, PE/ski gear, and uniforms (junior high)

---

## Child Allowance

- Your child must be **living in Japan** to receive Child Allowance
- Not available if the child will stay less than 1 year
- No allowance for non-working parents
- Apply directly at the City Office
