# @IUJ On-Campus Student Information Page

## Overview

The **@IUJ** page (`www2.iuj.ac.jp/atiuj/`) is IUJ's central hub for current students — a single page linking to every major student service, resource, and schedule. It is accessible from the MyIUJ! portal via the **@IUJ** link in the top navigation bar, or directly at the URL above.
