# Japanese Laws & Legal Advice

For support on matters concerning Japanese laws and legal advice, the OSS provides the following resources.

## Key Resources

- **Japanese Police Department (English):** [https://www.npa.go.jp/english/index.html](https://www.npa.go.jp/english/index.html) — drug-related issues, general police information
- **Ministry of Justice — Japanese Law Translations:** [https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/)
- **Consultation Services PDF (IUJ-created):** [https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/living/assistance.pdf](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/living/assistance.pdf) — helplines, advice on Japan's legal systems (Houterasu), etc.
- **Human Rights for Foreigners in Japan (Ministry of Justice):** [http://www.moj.go.jp/JINKEN/jinken21.html](http://www.moj.go.jp/JINKEN/jinken21.html) — covers discrimination, bullying, exclusion; PDFs available in multiple languages. [English version PDF](http://www.moj.go.jp/content/001281977.pdf)
- **Garbage Disposal Laws:** [Minamiuonuma City guidelines](http://www.city.minamiuonuma.niigata.jp/ikkrwebBrowse/material/files/group/36/26697_58330_misc.pdf) — **Off-campus students take particular note**
- **Comprehensive Support Handout (IUJ-created):** [https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/legalservices.pdf](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss-f/pdf/legalservices.pdf) — covers legal services, domestic violence, suicide prevention, counseling, and other support services for international people in Japan. Also available in hard copy at the OSS.
