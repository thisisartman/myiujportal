# Counseling Services

IUJ offers free, confidential counseling to all students and their family members throughout the academic year.

## About the Service

The counseling room is a space to talk about anything that impacts your learning and wellbeing at IUJ — culture shock, study skills, self-esteem, personal concerns, or anything on your mind. Sessions are **free of charge** and completely **confidential**: your identity and any information shared with the counselor are protected.

Both in-person and **Zoom sessions** are available.
