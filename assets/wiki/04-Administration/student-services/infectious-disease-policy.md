# School Infectious Disease Prevention Policy

IUJ follows Japan's official **School Infectious Disease Prevention Policy** under the **School Health and Safety Act** and the **Infectious Diseases Control Law**.

## Suspension of Attendance

If diagnosed with an infectious disease, absences are recorded as a **Suspension of Attendance** — not a regular absence. This protects your academic record.

## Return-to-Campus Guidelines

| Disease | Minimum Stay-Home Period | Return Condition |
|---------|--------------------------|------------------|
| **COVID-19** | 5 days from symptom onset | 24 hours symptom-free |
| **Influenza** | 5 days from symptom onset | 48 hours fever-free |

For the full list of applicable diseases: [School Infectious Disease PDF](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/School-infectious-disease.pdf)

## If You Are Infected

1. **Do not attend** any in-person university activities
2. **Notify your lecturers** as soon as possible
3. **Notify the Health and Wellness Coordinator** as soon as possible

Contact: healness@iuj.ac.jp | ext. 170
