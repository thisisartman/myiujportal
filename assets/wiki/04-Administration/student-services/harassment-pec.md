# Harassment & Discrimination — Professional Ethics Committee (PEC)

IUJ's **Professional Ethics Committee (PEC)** handles all cases of harassment and discrimination on campus. Guidelines last revised **April 2026**.

## Policy

IUJ prohibits any speech or behavior that causes discomfort or interferes with study, research, campus life, or a smooth workplace environment. This includes all forms of discrimination or harassment based on sex, race, religion, age, sexual orientation, or disability.

## PEC Activities

- Workshops on respect, professionalism, harassment, and discrimination
- Guest speakers on cultural communication, workplace ethics, sexual harassment awareness
- Training programs for handling harassment cases
- Quick, fair, and **confidential** assistance for complaints

## How to Get Help

Cases can be handled **informally** (through Informal Complaint Advisers) or **formally** (through a Formal Investigations Committee). The PEC handles matters quickly, fairly, and confidentially.
