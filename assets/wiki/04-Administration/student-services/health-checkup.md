# Annual Health Check-up for Full-time Students

Annual health checkups are **required under the School Health and Safety Act** and are provided free for all full-time IUJ students.

## Schedule

- Checkups are offered **twice a year**: in **April** and **October**
- Each student is eligible for **one checkup per academic year**
- New students are strongly recommended to attend during **Fall Term of their first year**

## Dates (recurring pattern)
Specific dates are announced each term — typically two days are offered in October (e.g., a Friday and a Tuesday), both 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. Watch for OSS announcements.

## Important

If you miss the IUJ-organised checkup, you must arrange and pay for a **private health checkup** at your own expense — approximately **¥15,000**.

Schedule in advance and do not miss it.
