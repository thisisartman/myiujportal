# MyIUJ! Student Portal

## Overview

**MyIUJ!** is IUJ's web-based student information portal, hosted at `www2.iuj.ac.jp`. It provides access to personal academic information, course registration, monthly expense statements, network file storage, and campus IT resources.

The portal has two main sections:

- **FrontPage** — Personal info, expense statements, curriculum links, and campus IT guides
- **Course (Dream Campus)** — Course registration system (separate ASP.NET application)

---

## Accessing the Portal

**Login URL:** [https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/myiuj/login/](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/myiuj/login/)

1. Navigate to the login URL above
2. Click **"Enter MyIUJ!"**
3. Enter your IUJ **email credentials** (same username/password as your IUJ email account)

> Passwords are case-sensitive. If you cannot log in, contact [postmaster@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:postmaster@iuj.ac.jp).

---

## FrontPage Section

After logging in, the FrontPage displays:

- **Your profile:** Name, Student ID, Program, School
- **Student Links:**
  - My Curriculum (GSIR) — links to `iuj.ac.jp/gsir/student_info/`
  - My Curriculum (GSIM) — links to `iuj.ac.jp/im-info/`
  - Statement of your expenses — monthly financial statements
- **MyIUJ! News** — campus announcements and notices
- **Network Folders (iuj-home)** — access to shared network storage

### Network Folder Access Guides

PDF guides are available directly from the FrontPage:

| Guide | Description |
|---|---|
| Dream Campus Manual for Students | How to use the course registration system |
| How to access iuj-home from mobile devices | iPhone/Android access |
| Creating a shortcut of "iuj-home" | Windows setup guide |

---

## Course Section (Dream Campus)

The **Course** tab links to **Dream Campus**, IUJ's academic affairs and course registration system. This is a separate application from the main MyIUJ! portal.

Use Dream Campus for:
- Course registration each term
- Viewing your academic record
- Checking grades and enrollment status

Refer to the **Dream Campus Manual for Students** (linked from the FrontPage) for full instructions.

---

## Session & Security

- **Session timeout:** The session disconnects automatically after **60 minutes** of inactivity. You will be prompted to log in again.
- **Log out properly:** Always click the **Logout** button (top-right of the screen) when finished — especially on shared/lab computers.
- **Personal data warning:** The portal displays personal academic and financial information. Do not leave a session unattended in a computer lab.
