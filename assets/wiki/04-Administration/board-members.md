# Board Members

The International University of Japan (IUJ) is a graduate university established with broad and strong support from Japan's business sector, academic institutions, and local communities. The founding Chairperson of the Board of Trustees was Mr. Sohei Nakayama, who played a central role in the university's establishment. Since then, distinguished leaders from the forefront of business and academia have successively served as Chairpersons, upholding the founding vision.

## Chairperson of the Board of Trustees

**Fumiya Kokubu**
Former Chairperson, Advisors, Marubeni Corporation

**Career:**
- Born in 1952
- Mar. 1975: Graduated from Keio University, Faculty of Economics
- Apr. 1975: Joined Marubeni Corporation
- Apr. 2005: Executive Officer
- Apr. 2008: Managing Executive Officer
- Jun. 2008: Representative Director & Managing Executive Officer
- Apr. 2010: Senior Executive Officer
- Apr. 2012: Executive Vice President
- Jun. 2012: Representative Director & Executive Vice President
- Apr. 2013: President & CEO
- Apr. 2019: Chairperson of the Board
- Apr. 2025: Honorary Advisor to the Board
- Jun. 2025: Honorary Advisor (current)
- Jun. 2025: Chairperson of International University of Japan (current)

**Major Organizational Roles:**
- Chairperson, Japan Foreign Trade Council (May 2022 – May 2024)
- Member, APEC Business Advisory Council (ABAC Japan) (Jul. 2021 – Jan. 2025)
- Chairperson, Japan Machinery Center for Trade and Investment (May 2022 – present)
- Chairperson, Keidanren Special Committee on Economic Reconstruction for Ukraine (Jun. 2023 – present)

## Representative Executive Trustee

**Akira Kimura**

- Graduated from the University of Tokyo, Faculty of Economics, 1982
- Completed PMD at Harvard Business School, 1994
- Served at Mitsui & Co., Japan Foreign Trade Council, and the University of Tokyo before assuming current position
