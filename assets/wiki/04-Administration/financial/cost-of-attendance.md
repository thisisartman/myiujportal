# Cost of Attendance and Budgeting

This page provides estimated annual costs for a single student (without family). All figures are in Japanese Yen (¥). Actual costs vary by lifestyle and travel habits.

---

## Annual Budget Estimates

| Expense | Amount |
|---------|--------|
| **Admissions Fee** | ¥300,000 (one-time, first year only) |
| **Tuition — 2-year program** | ¥3,200,000 per academic year |
| **Tuition — 1-year program** | ¥3,900,000 per academic year |
| Dormitory fees | ¥384,000–¥468,000/year (¥32,000–¥39,000/month) |
| Dormitory Maintenance Fee (move-in) | ¥10,000 (one-time) |
| Utilities | ¥60,000/year (¥5,000/month) |
| Meals | ¥360,000/year (¥30,000/month) |
| Books & Academic Supplies — GSIR | ¥30,000/year |
| Books & Academic Supplies — GSIM | ¥90,000/year |
| GSO-EC student fees | ¥4,500 |
| Special Program Insurance & Medical Assistance | ¥5,000 |
| National Health Insurance | ¥30,500 |
| Social & Entertainment | ¥120,000/year (¥10,000/month) |

### Estimated First-Year Total

| Program | Estimated Cost |
|---------|---------------|
| 2-year GSIR | ¥4,504,000–¥4,588,000 |
| 2-year GSIM | ¥4,564,000–¥4,648,000 |
| 1-year GSIR | ¥5,204,000–¥5,288,000 |
| 1-year GSIM | ¥5,264,000–¥5,348,000 |

> 2nd year costs are ¥300,000 less (no admissions fee). Travel expenses are not included.

## Scholarships

Most IUJ students receive some form of scholarship support. See the [IUJ Scholarships page](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/admis/scholarship/) for full eligibility details.

**Available scholarship types:**
- **IUJ Scholarships** — 4 tiers ranging from full tuition waiver to 30% tuition waiver
- **Partner/Government Scholarships** — JICA, MEXT, ADB, JERA, IMF, KMMF, AEON 1% Club (full scholarships)
- **JASSO Monthly Stipend** — ¥48,000/month, October–March (competitive renewal available for Year 2)
- **Post-arrival foundations** — Monthly stipends available after arrival; Japanese language skills typically required to apply

---

## Part-Time Work

From Spring Term of your first year, limited on-campus opportunities are available:

- Library staff (work permit required)
- Computer help desk (work permit required)
- Facilitators for IUJ non-degree programs
- Teaching Assistant (TA)
- Research Assistant (RA)

**Pay:** ¥800–¥1,000/hour. Maximum 28 hours/week with a work permit.

> **Important:** Many scholarships (JICA, ADB, IMF, etc.) prohibit work permit employment. Check your scholarship terms before applying for any paid position.

Off-campus work is very limited and requires a work permit.

---

## Spouse Employment

A dependent visa is for spousal support of the student's academic life — not for the purpose of finding employment. If a spouse works, they must obtain their own work permit and are limited to 28 hours/week. Spouse work arrangements must never interfere with the IUJ student's studies or class schedule.

---

## Family Budgeting

Students with families should consult the **[IUJ Family Guide](http://www.iuj.ac.jp/oss)** for a real-student budget example covering a household of 2 adults and 1 child. Additional costs for school-age children (uniforms, supplies, school trips) also apply.
