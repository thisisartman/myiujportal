# Accounting Information & Forms

## Overview

This page provides forms and documents related to tuition payment, campus departure notices, and bank remittance information. All forms require submission of a **hard copy to the Accounting Office** after filling in.

---

## Available Documents

| Document | Format |
|---|---|
| List of items charged as monthly expenses | Web / PDF |
| Receipt request form (領収証発行依頼書) | Form |
| Notice of leaving campus — dorm residents | PDF |
| Notice of leaving campus — dorm residents | Excel |
| Notice on payment of final expenses — off-campus residents | PDF |
| Notice on payment of final expenses — off-campus residents | Excel |
| Information for incoming IUJ students | PDF |
| Bank Information for Overseas Remittance | PDF |
| Receiving Money from Overseas | PDF |

---

## Notes

- Submit hard copies of completed forms to the **Accounting Office**
- For questions about monthly charges, contact [keiri@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:keiri@iuj.ac.jp)
- See also: `monthly-expenses-statement.md` for how to download PDF expense statements from MyIUJ!
- See also: `payment-flywire.md` for international payment via Flywire
