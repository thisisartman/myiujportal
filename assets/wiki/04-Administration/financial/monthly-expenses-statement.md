# Monthly Expenses Statement

## Overview

Students can download monthly PDF statements of their expenses directly from the MyIUJ! portal. Statements cover tuition, textbooks, printing charges, and other fees billed to your student account.

---

## How to Obtain Your Statement

1. Log in to MyIUJ! at [https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/myiuj/login/](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/myiuj/login/)
2. From the **Front Page**, click **"Statement of your expenses"** in the left sidebar
3. Choose a **Year and Month** from the dropdown list (listed in descending order, going back to September 2025)
4. Click **"Obtain Monthly Statement"**
5. The PDF opens in a new window

> **Requirement:** You must have **Adobe Acrobat Reader with Japanese fonts** installed to view the statement correctly.

---

## Charges Covered

Monthly statements include charges from three offices:

| Category | Responsible Office | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| General fees (tuition, housing, etc.) | Office of General Affairs (OGA) — Accounting Division | [keiri@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:keiri@iuj.ac.jp) |
| Textbook charges | Office of Academic Affairs (OAA) | [ofcgsir@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:ofcgsir@iuj.ac.jp) / [ofcgsim@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:ofcgsim@iuj.ac.jp) |
| Printing charges | MLIC IT Section | [mlic-com@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:mlic-com@iuj.ac.jp) |

---

## Important: Late Payment

> If you fail to make a payment by the due date, you will be charged a **late fee**.

Check your monthly statement regularly and pay by the stated deadline. Contact [keiri@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:keiri@iuj.ac.jp) if you have questions about your charges.
