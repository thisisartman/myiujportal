# JP Bank — Transferring Money Notice

> **Important notice for students using Japan Post Bank (JP Bank) accounts.**

JP Bank has specific procedures and restrictions for international money transfers. Students should check directly with OSS or the JP Bank website for current instructions before attempting to send or receive large sums internationally.

Source page: https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/jpbank/

Contact OSS at oss@iuj.ac.jp for specific guidance.
