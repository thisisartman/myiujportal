# Payment

## Payment Types

| Japanese | English |
|---|---|
| 入学検定料 | Application fee |
| 入学金 | Admission fee |
| 授業料 | Tuition fee |
| その他 | Other |

> Note the kanji above — you will need to recognize them on the Flywire payment site.

**Alumni using Flywire:** Enter `NA` in the Student ID field if you don't remember your ID.

---

## Payment Options

### Option 1 — Wire Transfer (Direct to IUJ)

Transfer directly to the IUJ bank account. Bank details are provided separately (see `jp-bank-transfers.md`).

### Option 2 — Flywire (International Payments)

IUJ has partnered with **Flywire** for secure international payments. Recommended for students paying from outside Japan.

**Why Flywire:**
- Pay from any country and any bank in your home currency
- Excellent foreign exchange rates, no hidden fees, Best Price Guaranteed
- Track payment progress online with email and text alerts
- Multilingual customer support available any time

**How to pay:** Select your country and payment method on the Flywire page linked from the IUJ payment portal.
