# About IUJ

## Learning from the World and Japan, Living with Local Community
### International University of Japan — Shaping the Future with the World

## International University of Japan (IUJ) is

1. **Japan's first graduate school university** offering all programs **in English**, where **students from over 60 countries** study together.
2. With half of the faculty coming from overseas, **small class sizes** enable close communication and advanced learning.
3. **Life on a fully residential campus** fosters **intercultural understanding**, a global perspective, and leadership skills.
4. Through close collaboration with the Minami-Uonuma region, IUJ promotes engagement with the local community and participation in community-based activities, **fostering human resources who embody a strong sense of regional identity**.
5. Generous financial support, including **fully funded scholarships** such as the Sohei Nakayama Memorial Scholarship and **external scholarships**, reduces financial burdens and creates an accessible learning environment for motivated students.

### Founding Principles
The founding philosophy of IUJ differs from that of traditional graduate schools focused on training researchers, aiming instead to cultivate highly skilled professionals who can play active and practical roles in the international community. Through interdisciplinary education conducted in English, IUJ fosters both professional expertise and global competence within a fully residential campus where students from diverse nationalities and backgrounds live and study together.

### History of IUJ
The concept of IUJ originated in the late 1970s with strong support from leaders in Japan's business and academic communities, and the University was established as a graduate-level university dedicated to developing highly skilled professionals capable of active engagement in the global arena.

### Education
The education envisioned by IUJ is designed to nurture individuals who can contribute meaningfully to the international community through a structured and forward-looking academic framework that integrates advanced expertise with practical competence.
