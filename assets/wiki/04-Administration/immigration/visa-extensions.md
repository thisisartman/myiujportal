# Visa Extensions (Student Visa)

## When to Apply

Submit your application **no earlier than 3 months before your visa expiry date**. Applying too early risks your supporting documents being considered outdated. Do not wait until the last moment either.

## Documents Required

Obtain from the immigration office website:
- **Application Form** (soft copy) — download from [Immigration Services Agency](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/student.html?hl=en)
- Soft copy of passport identification page
- Bust photo taken within 6 months (file size under 50 KB)

Obtain from IUJ:
- **Transcript** — apply at OAA
- **Certificate of Enrollment** — apply at OAA
- **Scholarship Certificate** — apply at OSS

Payment:
- **Fee: ¥6,000** in cash (paid at a later date)

## Information You Will Need to Provide

- Work history for the past 5 years (company name, address, dates)
- Total years of education from elementary school to present
- Scheduled graduation date
- Name of your supervisor
- Name of your research theme
- Number of credits earned in the past year
- Total credits earned since enrollment at IUJ
- Attendance rate at IUJ
