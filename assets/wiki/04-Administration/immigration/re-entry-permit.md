# Re-entry Permit

## During Your IUJ Program — Usually Not Required

IUJ students on a Student Visa do **not need a re-entry permit** during their program, provided:
- Your passport and Residence Card are valid
- You will re-enter Japan **within 1 year** of your departure

When leaving Japan, you use **Special Re-entry Permission** (automatic, declared at the airport).

## Critical Rules

- Special re-entry permission **cannot be extended while abroad**
- If you fail to return within 1 year, your **status of residence is lost**
- If your period of stay expires within 1 year of departure, return **before** your period of stay expires — not just within 1 year
- **You must carry your Residence Card** when departing — if you leave without it, you cannot use Special Re-entry Permission

## When a Formal Re-entry Permit IS Required

You must obtain a formal re-entry permit from the Immigration Office **before departing** if:
- You plan to leave Japan for **more than 1 year**, or
- You change your visa status to "Designated Activities" after graduation

The permit is issued immediately upon application at the Immigration Office.

## Warning

Leaving Japan without declaring Special Re-entry Permission results in:
- Your **Residence Card being confiscated** at the airport
- Your **National Health Insurance being automatically cancelled**

Contact OSS at oss@iuj.ac.jp for questions.
