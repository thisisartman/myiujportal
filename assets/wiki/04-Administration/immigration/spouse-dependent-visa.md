# Spouse & Dependent Visa (Certificate of Eligibility)

The student is legally responsible for all immigration procedures on behalf of their spouse and family. IUJ cannot be involved in private family affairs; consult your embassy in Japan directly for guidance.

## Prerequisites

- Student must establish residency in **appropriate housing** — off-campus if children are involved
- Student must have **sufficient funds** to support the family
- Spouses can obtain a work permit, but their purpose for coming must be to **support the student**, not for employment

## Recommended Timeline

Apply for the Dependent's Visa in **December** → processed during Winter Term (3–4 months) → family arrives **late March or early April**.

## Required Documents

| Document | Source |
|----------|--------|
| Application Form | [Immigration website](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/status/dependent.html?hl=en) |
| 1 photo of applicant (4cm × 3cm, white/light blue bg, within 6 months) | You |
| Proof of relationship (family registration / marriage cert / birth cert) | You |
| Copy of your passport + applicant's passport | You |
| Copy of Resident Record (Juumin hyou) — ¥250/sheet | City Office |
| Guarantee Letter (stating you cover all expenses + repatriation) | You (form at OSS) |
| Explanation Letter (why/how long + living conditions) | You |
| Certificate of Enrollment | OAA |
| Official Transcript | OAA |
| Scholarship Award Certificate | OSS or Scholarship Provider |
| Bank Statement + Bank Book copies | You |
| Return envelope + ¥470 stamp | You |

**Photo requirements:** 4cm × 3cm, within 6 months, white or light blue background, no shadows or decorations, professionally printed. Write applicant name on back.

**All foreign-language documents must be translated into Japanese.**

## Processing Time

Minimum **3 months**, maximum **4 months**. Calling immigration will not speed up the process.

> Always confirm procedures with your home embassy in Japan.

## Notes

- If relatives are providing living expenses, additional documents are required (oath of support, employment certificate, remittance evidence, bank statements)
- Submission must be in person or by a designated proxy (proxy must be a relative and show ID)
