# Leave of Absence & Withdrawal — Immigration Notes

## (1) Leave of Absence

Under the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act, if you have not been engaged in activities related to your "Student" residence status for **more than 3 months**, your status of residence **may be revoked** — unless there is a justifiable reason (e.g., health issues).

- If you must take a leave of absence, consult your academic advisor or a faculty member in charge of international students **before** doing so
- **Part-time work is not permitted during a leave of absence**

## (2) Graduation or Withdrawal

After graduating or withdrawing from IUJ, you are expected to **return to your home country** even if your Student status has not yet expired.

- If you need to remain in Japan temporarily to prepare for departure, **immediately change your status** from "Student" to "Temporary Visitor" or another appropriate status
- Continuing to stay in Japan under "Student" status after leaving IUJ is **not permitted**
