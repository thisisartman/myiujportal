# Alumni Mentor Networks

## Overview

IUJ runs two alumni mentor programs, coordinated jointly by Alumni Relations and Career Services. Both match current students with alumni in similar career fields for advice, guidance, and professional development.

---

## Global Women's Mentor Network

- Launched officially on **International Women's Day, March 8, 2009**
- Matches current female students with lady alumni in the same career field
- IUJ President Sugihara presented the logo at the symbolic opening ceremony
- The logo represents IUJ women worldwide guiding each other: the letter "W" encompasses a globe, with its last stroke reaching toward future opportunities

**How to join:** Contact Career Services staff or Alumni Relations staff to request a mentor.

---

## Men's Mentor Program

- Launched one year after the Women's Network (2010)
- Focuses on professional development for male students
- Matches students with alumni in similar professions

**Contact:** [gretchen@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:gretchen@iuj.ac.jp)

---

## Notes

- Both programs are open to current IUJ full-time students
- Mentors are IUJ alumni volunteering their time
- Students initiate by approaching Career Services or Alumni Relations
