# Career Support

IUJ provides comprehensive career support to help students plan and develop their professional paths through career advising, skill-building programs, and employer engagement.

## Services Offered

- Individual Career Counseling
- Entry Sheet, Resume (Japanese/English), and CV Review & Feedback
- Email updates on job opportunities and career events
- On-campus company information sessions
- Career seminars

**Contact:** [IUJ Career Support Office — Inquiry Form](https://forms.gle/yrjgAHJxHG2gsC329)

## Job Hunting Schedule

### 2-Year Program

| Period | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| Sep Year 1 (Enrollment) | Career Guidance seminar |
| Oct–Nov Year 1 (Fall Term) | Resume writing; company research begins |
| Dec–Mar Year 1 (Winter & Spring Terms) | Internship info sessions; interviews |
| Jul–Sep Year 1 (Summer Break) | Internship |
| Sep–Nov Year 2 (Fall Term) | Resume update |
| Dec–Feb Year 2 (Winter Term) | Job info sessions; interviews; offer |
| Mar–Jun Year 2 (Spring Term) | Preparation for work |

### 1-Year Program

| Period | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| Sep (Enrollment) | Career Guidance |
| Oct–Dec (Fall Term) | Resume writing |
| Jan–Mar (Winter Term) | Info sessions |
| Mar–Jun (Spring–Capstone) | Interviews; offer |

### Employment Start Date

- **New Graduates:** Fall intake (September of graduation year) or April of the following year, depending on company
- **Mid-Career:** Start date negotiated with employer

## Annual Career Event Calendar

| Month | Event |
|-------|-------|
| September | Career Guidance (Job Hunting Mindset) |
| October | Career Seminar #1 — English Resume Writing; Career Seminar #2 — Japan Job Hunting System & Recruitment Practices |
| November | Career Seminar #3 — Self-Analysis & Company Research; Japanese Business Manner Seminar; Japanese Resume Writing Support |
| December | JLPT-Level Mock Interviews in Japanese |
| January | Company Info Sessions (Job positions) |
| February | Alumni Networking Event (Private sector, International Organizations, PhD speakers); Company Info Sessions |
| March–May | Company Info Sessions (Jobs & Internships) |
