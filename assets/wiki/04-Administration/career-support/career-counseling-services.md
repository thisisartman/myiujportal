# Career Counseling and Services (CC&S)

## Overview

Career Counseling and Services (CC&S) supports IUJ full-time students in their job and internship search, starting **before they even arrive on campus**. Services include Facebook support groups, email advice, resume critiques, workshops, career development classes, and 1-on-1 coaching.

> Exchange students are not eligible for CC&S services.

**Contact:** [ccs@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:ccs@iuj.ac.jp)

---

## Services Offered

- 1-on-1 counseling and job/internship hunt planning
- Information via bulletin boards, IUJ LAN, email, career library, and web
- **IUJ Resume Book** — student resumes distributed to key contacts each October; available online Nov–June
- Resume and cover letter critiques
- Internship placement assistance
- Fall workshop series
- Career library resources
- Subscriptions to **CareerLeader.com**
- Networking with alumni
- Mentor Programs for men and women (see Alumni Mentor Networks)
- **A-CAN** (Alumni Career Advice Network) — connect with alumni for career advice

## Career Development Before Campus

IUJ provides proactive pre-arrival career support including guidance on career readiness skills. Prospective students with career questions can contact [ccs@iuj.ac.jp](mailto:ccs@iuj.ac.jp).
