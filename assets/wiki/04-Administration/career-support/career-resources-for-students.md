## Job Hunting Schedule

### 2-Year Program

| Phase | 1st Year | 2nd Year |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Fall Term | Career Guidance, Resume Writing | Resume Update |
| Winter Term | Company Research | Job Info Sessions, Interviews, Offers |
| Spring Term | Internship Info Sessions, Interviews | Preparation for working |
| Summer | Internship | — |

### 1-Year Program

| Sep–Nov | Dec–Feb | Mar–Jun |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Career Guidance, Resume Writing | Info Sessions | Interviews, Offers, Preparation |

### Employment Start Dates
- **New Graduates:** Fall intake in year of graduation, or April intake the following year (varies by company)
- **Mid-Career Recruitment:** Agreed directly with employer

## Status of Residence After Graduation

After graduation, a student residence status becomes invalid even if the expiry date has not passed. If you plan to stay in Japan for work or job hunting, you must change your status.

[Full details on post-graduation visa procedures](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/career/currentstudents/visaaftergrad/)

## Business Cards for Job Hunting

IUJ-format business cards (English and Japanese, one per side) can be ordered through the **IUJ School Shop**. Cost: **¥3,200**.

**How to order:**
1. Download the application form for your school and fill it out on your PC (not by hand)
   - GSIR: [English (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/EN_IR_Business%20Card.pdf) | [Japanese (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/JP_IR_Business%20Card.pdf)
   - GSIM: [English (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/EN_IM_Business%20Card.pdf) | [Japanese (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/JP_IM_Business%20Card.pdf)
2. Print and bring to the School Shop
3. Proof image ready in ~3 business days — visit to review
4. Cards printed and delivered ~3 business days after approval — pick up and pay ¥3,200
