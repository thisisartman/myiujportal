# Education

## Educating Future Global Leaders

**Educating leaders who can contribute to the development for various countries of the world as well as to their international cooperation.**

## IUJ Future Vision

In 2021, IUJ launched a president-led Future Vision Project and formulated the IUJ Future Vision toward 2030, outlining concrete directions for education and research, student support, and engagement with the local community. This Vision clearly defines the kind of university IUJ aspires to become and the areas in which it seeks to create value.

[Read more about IUJ Future Plan](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/vision/)

## Visions and Strengths of Human Resource (HR) Development

IUJ aims to cultivate individuals who can confront complex and diverse challenges and take action toward solutions across differences in countries, organizations, and positions. Going beyond the acquisition of specialized knowledge, its education emphasizes dialogue, deep reflection, and engagement with people from diverse backgrounds and values within a campus surrounded by rich nature, where classroom learning and daily interactions broaden students' perspectives and deepen their understanding of others.

Through these experiences, students develop the judgment, communication, and practical skills required in the international community.

[Read more](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/strength/)

## Mission on Nurturing Human Resources and Three Policies

The objectives of IUJ are to:
- Teach and conduct research in academic theory and application necessary for practical solutions of issues facing international society and international businesses, and
- Foster global leaders who have a high level of professional knowledge and skills with deep understanding of and respect for different cultures, thereby contributing to the development of international society.

The mission on nurturing human resources and the three policies of each graduate school:
- [GSIR Mission and Three Policies](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/ir-policy/)
- [GSIM Mission and Three Policies](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/im-policy/)

## Quality Assurance

IUJ continuously enhances the quality of education through the integration of internal quality assurance and external accreditation. IUJ has been accredited by the Japan Institution for Higher Education Evaluation (JIHEE), confirming compliance with national evaluation standards, while GSIM has earned AACSB accreditation, an internationally recognized mark of excellence in management education.

[Read more](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/accreditation/)

## Faculty Development

The quality of IUJ's education is underpinned by the teaching capabilities of faculty members, and at IUJ, educational quality is continuously enhanced through faculty development initiatives such as seminars focused on improving teaching practices and sharing effective methods.

[Read more](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/about/education/fd/)
