# Immigration Guide for Current Students

The Office of Student Services (OSS) assists students with all immigration-related matters. Contact OSS directly for help with any of the topics below.

---

## Entry Requirements: Biometric Data

All foreign nationals entering Japan must be **fingerprinted and photographed** at the port of entry. IUJ students are required to cooperate. Refusal (by those not exempt) results in denial of entry.

**Exemptions:**
- Special permanent residents
- Persons under age 16
- Diplomats or Official status holders
- Persons invited by heads of administrative organs

---

## Key Immigration Topics

### Visa Extensions
Student visas must be extended before expiry. Contact OSS well in advance (at least 2–3 months before your visa expires). OSS will provide guidance on the process and required documents.
→ [Visa Extensions details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/visa-extensions/)

### Re-entry Permit
If you plan to leave Japan and return, you must hold a valid re-entry permit (or use the special re-entry permission for short trips up to 1 year).
→ [Re-entry Permit details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/re-entry-permit/)

### Work Permit (Part-time)
Students on a Student Visa may work part-time with a **Permission to Engage in Activity other than that Permitted by the Status of Residence**. This must be obtained from the Immigration Bureau before beginning any work.
→ [Work Permit details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/work-permit/)

### Work Visa (After Graduation — Designated Stay)
Students actively job hunting who are registered with Career Services may be eligible for IUJ's support in obtaining a **Designated Stay Visa** after graduation. This visa supports continued job search activities in Japan.
- Must be actively job hunting before graduation to qualify for IUJ's support
→ [Work Visa details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/work-visa/)

### Leave of Absence
Students who need to temporarily interrupt their studies must formally apply for leave. OSS handles the paperwork and immigration implications.
→ [Leave of Absence details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/leave-of-absence/)

### Spouse Visas
Spouses of IUJ students may be eligible for a Dependent Visa. OSS can advise on requirements and the application process.
→ [Spouse Visa details](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/spouse-visas/)

## Niigata Immigration Bureau

Immigration matters for IUJ students are handled through the Niigata Immigration Bureau. OSS can provide a map and assistance scheduling appointments.
→ [Map to Niigata Immigration Bureau](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/oss/current-students/issues/map-to-niigata/)
