# Transcripts & Certificates

IUJ can issue official certificates and transcripts to currently enrolled students upon written request. This page covers the types of certificates available, how to apply, fees, and receipt options.

> **Alumni:** See the [Alumni Transcripts page](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/alumni/transcripts/) for certificate requests after graduation.

---

## Types of Certificates Available

### Transcript
Records all courses taken, credits earned, grades received, and GPA. Available in **English or Japanese** (specify when applying).

### Certificate of Enrollment
Confirms current enrollment in a Master's or PhD program, including enrollment date and program name. Issued **bilingually (English + Japanese) only**.

### Certificate of Medium of Instruction
Confirms that IUJ's graduate programs are taught entirely in English. Available in **English or Japanese**.

### Certificate of Expected Graduation
Confirms expected graduation with a Master's degree, including expected graduation date and degree title. **Cannot be issued until:**
- 2-year Master's students: after completing course registration in the Fall term of Year 2
- 1-year program students: after completing course registration in the Spring term

Issued **bilingually (English + Japanese) only**.

---

## How to Apply

**Step 1 — Submit the Application Form**

Download and complete the [Certificate Application Form (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/certificate_applicationform_currentstudents.pdf), then email it as an attachment to your school office:

- **GSIR:** ofcgsir@iuj.ac.jp
- **GSIM:** ofcgsim@iuj.ac.jp

IUJ does not accept applications from anyone other than the certificate holder themselves.

**Step 2 — Payment**

The certificate-issuance fee is deducted from your Daishi Hokuetsu Bank account on the **25th of the month following your application** (or the 23rd/24th if the 25th falls on a weekend or national holiday).

**Step 3 — Processing Time**

Certificates are normally issued the **business day after** your application is accepted. Processing may take longer due to national holidays, weekends, or special closures (e.g., New Year: approx. December 25 – January 5).

*Business days exclude: Saturdays, Sundays, Japanese National Holidays, and the New Year Holiday.*

---

## How to Receive Your Certificate

**Option 1 — Pick up at OAA**
Collect your certificates in person from the Office of Academic Affairs.

**Option 2 — Postal delivery**
OAA staff will mail the certificates to your registered address. Delivery may be delayed in some circumstances.

> **Important:** IUJ does **not** issue electronic versions of certificates and will **not** send them by email.

---

## Certificate Verification

For third-party verification of IUJ certificates, use the [Certificate Verification portal](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/alumni/transcripts/#verification).
